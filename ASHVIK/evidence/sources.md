## Brand names

Brand examples in the knowledge base are **illustrative** (common Indian market names). They are not endorsements, and availability varies by state and stock. Always prescribe by generic name where possible.

## Retrieval notes

- ICMR STW Vol. 3 and Vol. 4 PDFs were downloaded and text-extracted into `raw/`.
- Vol. 3 covers diabetes, hypertension and other medical conditions; Vol. 4 covers surgical specialties.
- Infectious disease guidance (malaria, dengue, typhoid, diarrhoea) comes from NVBDCP/NCDC/WHO and is cross-checked in the condition files.
- Where a national guideline does not specify a brand or a dose, the file says **"verify locally"** instead of inventing a value.
