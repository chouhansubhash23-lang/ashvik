# Caregiver Support

> **Source:** NPHCE; WHO; NHP
> **Evidence:** `../../evidence/sources.md`
> **Last verified:** 2026-10-28
> **Status:** Draft — clinician review required
> **Setting:** Rural OPD / PHC (India)

## 1. Quick facts

- Caregivers are essential partners in care.
- Caregiver burnout is common and under-recognised.
- Assess burden, health, and finances.
- Provide respite and training.

## 2. Clinical features (caregiver)

- Fatigue, anxiety, depression
- Sleep disturbance
- Social isolation
- Financial strain
- Neglect of own health

## 3. Diagnosis / Assessment

| Tool | Use |
|---|---|
| Caregiver burden scale | Burden |
| PHQ-9/GAD-7 | Depression/anxiety |
| Social support | Isolation |
| Financial assessment | Need for help |
| Caregiving tasks | Training needs |

## 4. Treatment

| Measure | Detail |
|---|---|
| Education | Disease, medicines, red flags |
| Respite | Family/community support |
| Counselling | Stress management |
| Treat depression | SSRI if indicated |
| Paracetamol 500 mg | For caregiver's own pain/fever |

**Brand examples (illustrative):** Paracetamol — Dolo 650; Sertraline — Daxid.

## 5. Supportive care

- Support groups
- Financial schemes
- Home-based care
- Emergency plan

## 6. Drug interactions and cautions

- **SSRIs:** serotonin syndrome, bleeding.
- **Sedatives:** caregiver impairment.
- **Alcohol:** avoid.
- **Self-neglect:** screen.

## 7. Red flags — refer immediately

- Suicidal ideation
- Severe depression
- Caregiver collapse
- Elder/child abuse
- Financial crisis

## 8. Follow-up

- Regular check-ins.
- Respite planning.
- Mental health support.
