# Malaria Chemoprophylaxis

> **Source:** NVBDCP; WHO; NHP
> **Evidence:** `../../evidence/sources.md`
> **Last verified:** 2026-10-28
> **Status:** Draft — clinician review required
> **Setting:** Rural OPD / PHC (India)

## 1. Quick facts

- Prophylaxis for travellers/pregnant women/high-risk groups.
- Start before exposure and continue after.
- Use species/area-appropriate drug.
- Protection is not 100%.

## 2. Indications

- Travel to endemic area
- Pregnancy in endemic area
- Non-immune visitors
- Splenectomy/high risk (specialist)

## 3. Diagnosis / Assessment

| Assessment | Detail |
|---|---|
| Destination | Endemicity/resistance |
| Duration | Short/long |
| Pregnancy | Drug choice |
| G6PD | Primaquine risk |
| Renal/hepatic | Dose adjustment |

## 4. Treatment

| Drug | Dose | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| Chloroquine | 300 mg base weekly | Before/during/after |
| Mefloquine | 250 mg weekly | Before/during/after |
| Doxycycline | 100 mg daily | Start 1–2 days before, continue 4 weeks after |
| Atovaquone-proguanil | 250/100 mg daily | Start 1–2 days before, continue 7 days after |

**Brand examples (illustrative):** Lariago; Mefliam; Doxt; Malarone.

## 5. Supportive care

- Bed nets
- Repellents
- Adherence counselling

## 6. Drug interactions and cautions

- **Doxycycline:** avoid in pregnancy/children < 8 y.
- **Mefloquine:** neuropsychiatric effects.
- **Chloroquine:** QT/psoriasis.
- **G6PD:** avoid primaquine.

## 7. Red flags — refer immediately

- Fever during/after travel
- Severe malaria symptoms
- Pregnancy with malaria

## 8. Follow-up

- Complete prophylaxis.
- Test if fever.
