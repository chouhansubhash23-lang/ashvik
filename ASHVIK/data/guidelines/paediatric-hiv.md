# Paediatric HIV

> **Source:** NACO; WHO; NHP
> **Evidence:** `../../evidence/sources.md`
> **Last verified:** 2026-10-28
> **Status:** Draft — clinician review required
> **Setting:** Rural OPD / PHC (India)

## 1. Quick facts

- Early diagnosis and ART are life-saving.
- Prevent mother-to-child transmission (PMTCT).
- Test with consent/confidentiality.
- Link to ART centre.

## 2. Clinical features

- Failure to thrive
- Recurrent infections
- Chronic diarrhoea
- Lymphadenopathy
- Oral thrush
- Developmental delay

## 3. Diagnosis / Assessment

| Test | Use |
|---|---|
| HIV DNA PCR (< 18 months) | Early infant diagnosis |
| HIV rapid test (> 18 months) | Screening |
| CD4/viral load | Monitoring |
| TB screening | All children |
| Nutrition assessment | All children |

## 4. Treatment

| Situation | Action |
|---|---|
| Confirmed HIV | Link to ART centre; start ART |
| PMTCT | Maternal ART + infant prophylaxis |
| Opportunistic infection | Treat and refer |
| PEP | Within 72 h |

**ART regimens** are NACO-provided; do not start ad-hoc.

**Brand examples (illustrative):** Paediatric ART formulations; Cotrimoxazole 960 mg prophylaxis.

## 5. Supportive care

- Nutrition
- Adherence counselling
- Immunisation (avoid live vaccines in severe immunosuppression)
- Psychosocial support

## 6. Drug interactions and cautions

- **Rifampicin:** reduces some ART levels.
- **Cotrimoxazole:** rash, hyperkalaemia.
- **ART:** hepatotoxicity, renal toxicity.
- **Live vaccines:** avoid in severe immunosuppression.

## 7. Red flags — refer immediately

- Severe opportunistic infection
- Respiratory distress
- Altered consciousness
- Severe malnutrition
- Suspected IRIS

## 8. Follow-up

- ART adherence/viral load.
- TB screening.
- Growth monitoring.
