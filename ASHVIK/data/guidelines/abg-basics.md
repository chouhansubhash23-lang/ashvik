# Arterial Blood Gas (ABG) Basics

> **Source:** ICMR STW Vol. 3 (2022); WHO; NHP
> **Evidence:** `../../evidence/sources.md`
> **Last verified:** 2026-10-28
> **Status:** Draft — clinician review required
> **Setting:** Rural OPD / PHC (India)

## 1. Quick facts

- ABG assesses oxygenation, ventilation, and acid-base status.
- Normal: pH 7.35–7.45, PaO2 80–100 mmHg, PaCO2 35–45 mmHg, HCO3 22–26 mmol/L.
- Interpret systematically: pH → PaCO2 → HCO3 → compensation.
- Always correlate with clinical status.

## 2. Systematic interpretation

| Step | Question |
|---|---|
| 1 | pH acidemia or alkalemia? |
| 2 | PaCO2 — respiratory component? |
| 3 | HCO3 — metabolic component? |
| 4 | Compensation appropriate? |
| 5 | Oxygenation (PaO2, FiO2) |
| 6 | Anion gap (if metabolic acidosis) |

## 3. Common patterns

| Pattern | pH | PaCO2 | HCO3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Metabolic acidosis | ↓ | ↓ (compensatory) | ↓ |
| Metabolic alkalosis | ↑ | ↑ | ↑ |
| Respiratory acidosis | ↓ | ↑ | ↑ |
| Respiratory alkalosis | ↑ | ↓ | ↓ |

## 4. Diagnosis / Assessment

- Indications: respiratory distress, shock, DKA, renal failure, poisoning.
- Radial artery puncture (Allen test).
- Complications: hematoma, ischemia, infection.

## 5. Treatment implications

| Finding | Action |
|---|---|
| Hypoxaemia | Oxygen therapy |
| Hypercapnia | Ventilatory support/refer |
| Metabolic acidosis | Treat cause; sodium bicarbonate 50 mmol IV if severe |
| Hyperkalaemia | Calcium gluconate 10 mL of 10% IV; insulin-dextrose |

**Brand examples (illustrative):** Sodium bicarbonate; Calcium gluconate.

## 6. Drug interactions and cautions

- No specific drug interactions; correct electrolytes cautiously and avoid rapid shifts.
- Sodium bicarbonate may cause alkalosis and hypokalaemia.
- Oxygen therapy can cause CO2 retention in COPD.

## 7. Supportive care

- Monitor trends
- Correct electrolytes
- Treat underlying cause

## 8. Red flags — refer immediately

- pH < 7.20 or > 7.55
- PaO2 < 60 mmHg despite oxygen
- PaCO2 > 50 mmHg with acidosis
- Severe metabolic acidosis
- Suspected poisoning

## 9. Follow-up

- Repeat ABG after intervention.
- Document FiO2.
