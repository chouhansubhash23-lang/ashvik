# Fever with Rash — Differential Diagnosis

> **Source:** NVBDCP; NCDC; WHO; ICMR antimicrobial guidelines
> **Evidence:** `../../evidence/sources.md`
> **Last verified:** 2026-10-27
> **Status:** Draft — clinician review required
> **Setting:** Rural OPD / PHC (India)

## 1. Quick facts

- Fever with rash is common in monsoon/post-monsoon: dengue, chikungunya, measles, rubella, typhoid, scrub typhus, leptospirosis.
- **Do not give NSAIDs/aspirin** until dengue is excluded.
- Look for warning signs and treat as a syndrome while tests are pending.

## 2. Differential features

| Condition | Rash | Other clues |
|---|---|---|
| Dengue | Maculopapular, confluent, sparing palms/soles | Myalgia, thrombocytopenia, warning signs |
| Chikungunya | Maculopapular | Severe joint pain |
| Measles | Behind ears → face → trunk, Koplik spots | Cough, coryza, conjunctivitis |
| Rubella | Pink macules, lymphadenopathy | Mild fever |
| Typhoid | Rose spots (faint) | Step-ladder fever, abdominal pain |
| Scrub typhus | Maculopapular, eschar | Rural/forest exposure, fever |
| Leptospirosis | Maculopapular, jaundice | Flood/field exposure, muscle pain |
| Drug rash | Symmetrical, itchy | New drug, eosinophilia |

## 3. Diagnosis

| Test | Use |
|---|---|
| CBC + platelets | Dengue, typhoid |
| Dengue NS1/IgM | Dengue |
| Malaria RDT/smear | Malaria |
| Chikungunya IgM | Chikungunya |
| Scrub typhus IgM | Scrub typhus |
| Blood culture | Typhoid |
| Leptospira IgM | Leptospirosis |

## 4. Treatment principles

| Situation | Action |
|---|---|
| Suspected dengue | Paracetamol only; hydration; monitor warning signs |
| Suspected chikungunya | Paracetamol; joint pain management; avoid NSAIDs until dengue excluded |
| Suspected measles | Supportive; vitamin A; isolate; vaccinate contacts |
| Suspected scrub typhus | Doxycycline 100 mg BD x 7 days (adult) |
| Suspected leptospirosis | Doxycycline or penicillin; refer if severe |
| Suspected typhoid | See typhoid chapter |

**Brand examples (illustrative):** Paracetamol — Dolo 650; Doxycycline — Doxt.

## 5. Supportive care

- Hydration, rest, antipyretics
- Monitor for warning signs daily
- Isolation for measles/chickenpox
- Vector control and hygiene counselling

## 6. Drug interactions and cautions

- **NSAIDs/aspirin:** bleeding risk in dengue — avoid.
- **Doxycycline:** avoid in pregnancy/children < 8 years; photosensitivity; antacids reduce absorption.
- **Paracetamol:** max 4 g/day; hepatotoxicity.
- **Steroids:** not routine; may worsen infection.

## 7. Red flags — refer immediately

- Bleeding, severe abdominal pain, persistent vomiting (dengue)
- Altered consciousness, seizures
- Breathlessness, chest pain
- Jaundice with renal failure (leptospirosis)
- Hypotension/shock
- Pregnancy with fever and rash
- Infant with fever and rash

## 8. Follow-up

- Review in 24–48 h.
- Repeat CBC if dengue suspected.
- Notify measles/cholera as required.
