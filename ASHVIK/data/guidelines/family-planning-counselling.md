# Family Planning Counselling

> **Source:** National Family Planning Programme; WHO; NHP
> **Evidence:** `../../evidence/sources.md`
> **Last verified:** 2026-10-28
> **Status:** Draft — clinician review required
> **Setting:** Rural OPD / PHC (India)

## 1. Quick facts

- Offer a method mix and respect choice.
- Condoms protect against STI/HIV.
- Screen for contraindications.
- Postpartum and post-abortion counselling is key.

## 2. Methods

| Method | Notes |
|---|---|
| Condom | STI protection |
| Combined OCP | Daily; avoid smokers > 35 |
| Progestin-only pill | Breastfeeding-friendly |
| DMPA | Every 3 months |
| IUCD | 10 years |
| Sterilisation | Permanent |
| Emergency pill | Emergency only |

## 3. Diagnosis / Assessment

- Age, parity, breastfeeding
- Smoking, BP, migraine
- VTE history
- Drug interactions (rifampicin, AEDs)

## 4. Treatment

| Method | Dose/use |
|---|---|
| Combined OCP | 1 tablet daily |
| DMPA | 150 mg IM every 3 months |
| Emergency LNG | 1.5 mg within 72 h |
| IUCD | Insertion |

**Brand examples (illustrative):** Mala-N; Depo-Provera; i-pill; CuT 380A.

## 5. Supportive care

- Counselling
- Condom demonstration
- Follow-up
- Partner involvement

## 6. Drug interactions and cautions

- **Rifampicin/AEDs:** reduce OCP efficacy.
- **OCPs:** VTE, hypertension, migraine.
- **DMPA:** delayed fertility, weight gain.
- **IUCD:** cramping, expulsion.

## 7. Red flags — refer

- Suspected pregnancy
- Abnormal bleeding
- Severe pelvic pain
- DVT symptoms
- Breast lump

## 8. Follow-up

- Review after 3 months.
- Check IUCD threads.
- Method switching as needed.
