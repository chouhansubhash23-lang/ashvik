# Hernia (Inguinal, Umbilical, Incisional)

> **Source:** ICMR STW Vol. 3 (2022); WHO; NHP
> **Evidence:** `../../evidence/sources.md`
> **Last verified:** 2026-10-27
> **Status:** Draft — clinician review required
> **Setting:** Rural OPD / PHC (India)

## 1. Quick facts

- Hernia = protrusion of contents through a defect.
- Common: inguinal, umbilical, incisional, femoral.
- **Irreducible/tender hernia = emergency** (strangulation).
- Elective repair prevents complications.

## 2. Clinical features

- Swelling that increases on coughing/standing
- Reducible (goes back on lying down)
- Pain/discomfort
- Irreducible (cannot reduce)
- Strangulated: severe pain, tenderness, vomiting, no flatus, fever
- Obstructed: features of obstruction

## 3. Diagnosis

| Test | Use |
|---|---|
| Clinical exam | Reducibility, cough impulse |
| Ultrasound | Contents, complications |
| X-ray | Obstruction (if suspected) |
| CBC | Infection/strangulation |

## 4. Treatment

### 4.1 Reducible hernia

- Elective surgical referral.
- Avoid heavy lifting/straining; treat constipation/cough.
- Truss not recommended routinely.

### 4.2 Irreducible/strangulated

- **Emergency referral.**
- Nil by mouth, IV fluids, NG tube if obstruction.
- Analgesia (paracetamol 1 g); antibiotics (ceftriaxone 1 g IV + metronidazole 500 mg IV) if strangulation suspected.
- Do not force reduction.

**Brand examples (illustrative):** Ceftriaxone — Monocef; Metronidazole — Flagyl.

## 5. Supportive care

- Treat cough/constipation
- Weight management
- Smoking cessation
- Pre-op optimisation

## 6. Drug interactions and cautions

- **Opioids:** constipation (worsens straining).
- **NSAIDs:** avoid in dehydration.
- **Antibiotics:** renal adjustment.

## 7. Red flags — refer immediately

- Irreducible/tender hernia
- Strangulation (severe pain, fever, vomiting)
- Obstruction
- Skin changes over hernia
- Incarcerated hernia in children/elderly
- Femoral hernia (higher strangulation risk)

## 8. Follow-up

- Post-op wound review.
- Avoid heavy lifting for 4–6 weeks.
- Treat chronic cough/constipation.
