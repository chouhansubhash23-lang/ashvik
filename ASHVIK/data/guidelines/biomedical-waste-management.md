# Biomedical Waste Management

> **Source:** BMW Rules 2016 (India); CPCB; WHO
> **Evidence:** `../../evidence/sources.md`
> **Last verified:** 2026-10-28
> **Status:** Draft — clinician review required
> **Setting:** Rural OPD / PHC (India)

## 1. Quick facts

- Segregate waste at source into colour-coded bins.
- Never mix infectious and general waste.
- Sharps go into puncture-proof containers.
- Follow BMW Rules 2016 and local CPCB authorization.

## 2. Diagnosis / Assessment

- Review segregation practices.
- Check colour-coded bins and labels.
- Check storage area and pickup records.
- Assess staff training.

## 3. Treatment: segregation

| Colour | Waste |
|---|---|
| Yellow | Infectious, anatomical, soiled |
| Red | Contaminated recyclable plastic |
| White | Sharps (needles, blades) |
| Blue | Glass, metallic implants |

- Use **10 mL of 1% sodium hypochlorite** for spills.
- Label bins with biohazard symbol.

## 4. Drug interactions and cautions

- **Dose:** not applicable (non-drug topic).
- **Sodium hypochlorite:** corrosive; avoid mixing with acids.
- **Sharps:** never recap; dispose immediately.
- **Cytotoxic waste:** separate yellow bin with label.

## 5. Supportive care

- Train staff.
- Provide PPE.
- Maintain registers.
- Tie bags when 3/4 full.

## 6. Red flags

- Mixed waste
- Sharps in general waste
- Overfilled bins
- No pickup/logs
- Needlestick exposure

## 7. Follow-up

- Monthly audit.
- Report to pollution control board.
- Refresh training.
