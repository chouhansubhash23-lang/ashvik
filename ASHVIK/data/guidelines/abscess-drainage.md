# Abscess Drainage

> **Source:** ICMR STW Vol. 3 (2022); WHO; NHP
> **Evidence:** `../../evidence/sources.md`
> **Last verified:** 2026-10-28
> **Status:** Draft — clinician review required
> **Setting:** Rural OPD / PHC (India)

## 1. Quick facts

- **Incision and drainage (I&D) is the primary treatment** for abscess.
- Antibiotics alone do not cure a collection.
- Use local anaesthesia; drain pus completely; pack if cavity is large.
- Send pus for culture in severe/recurrent cases.

## 2. Clinical features

- Tender, fluctuant, warm swelling
- Erythema, pain
- Fever (if cellulitis/systemic)
- Pus pointing

## 3. Diagnosis / Assessment

| Test | Use |
|---|---|
| Clinical exam | Fluctuance |
| Ultrasound | Deep abscess |
| Pus culture | Recurrent/severe |
| Blood sugar | Diabetes |

## 4. Treatment: I&D steps

| Step | Detail |
|---|---|
| 1 | Consent and local anaesthesia (lidocaine 1–2%) |
| 2 | Incise over the most dependent point |
| 3 | Drain pus; break loculations |
| 4 | Irrigate with saline |
| 5 | Pack cavity (if large) |
| 6 | Dressing; review in 24–48 h |
| 7 | Antibiotics if cellulitis/systemic signs |

### Antibiotics (if indicated)

| Drug | Dose |
|---|---|
| Amoxicillin-clavulanate | 625 mg TDS x 5–7 days |
| Cephalexin | 500 mg QID x 5–7 days |
| Clindamycin (penicillin allergy) | 300–450 mg TDS |

**Brand examples (illustrative):** Augmentin; Sporidex; Dalacin; Lidocaine — Xylocaine.

## 5. Supportive care

- Analgesia (paracetamol 500 mg)
- Elevation
- Warm compresses
- Diabetes control

## 6. Drug interactions and cautions

- **Lidocaine:** maximum dose; aspirate before injecting.
- **Antibiotics:** allergy, renal adjustment.
- **NSAIDs:** bleeding, kidney injury.
- **Diabetes:** poor healing; control sugar.

## 7. Red flags — refer immediately

- Abscess in face/neck/hand
- Systemic signs/sepsis
- Necrotising infection
- Deep/abdominal abscess
- Immunocompromised/diabetes
- No improvement after drainage

## 8. Follow-up

- Review in 24–48 h.
- Repack if needed.
- Remove pack when cavity closes.
- Wound care education.
