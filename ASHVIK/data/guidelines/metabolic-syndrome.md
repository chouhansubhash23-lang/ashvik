# Metabolic Syndrome

> **Source:** ICMR; WHO; NPCDCS
> **Evidence:** `../../evidence/sources.md`
> **Last verified:** 2026-10-28
> **Status:** Draft — clinician review required
> **Setting:** Rural OPD / PHC (India)

## 1. Quick facts

- Cluster of central obesity, dysglycaemia, dyslipidaemia, hypertension.
- Increases diabetes and cardiovascular risk.
- Lifestyle is first-line.
- Treat each component.

## 2. Clinical features

- Central obesity
- High BP
- High sugar
- High triglycerides/low HDL
- Fatty liver
- Acanthosis nigricans

## 3. Diagnosis / Assessment

| Component | Cut-off |
|---|---|
| Waist | ≥ 90 cm men, ≥ 80 cm women (Asian) |
| BP | ≥ 130/85 |
| Fasting glucose | ≥ 100 mg/dL |
| Triglycerides | ≥ 150 mg/dL |
| HDL | < 40 men, < 50 women |

Three or more = metabolic syndrome.

## 4. Treatment

| Component | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Obesity | Diet/exercise |
| Hypertension | Amlodipine/telmisartan |
| Dysglycaemia | Metformin 500 mg BD |
| Dyslipidaemia | Statin |
| Fatty liver | Weight loss |

**Brand examples (illustrative):** Glycomet; Telma; Atorva.

## 5. Supportive care

- Dietitian
- Exercise 150 min/week
- Sleep
- Smoking cessation

## 6. Drug interactions and cautions

- **Metformin:** GI upset; hold before contrast.
- **Statins:** myopathy.
- **Antihypertensives:** hypotension.
- **Alcohol:** worsens liver.

## 7. Red flags — refer immediately

- Chest pain
- Severe hyperglycaemia
- Pancreatitis
- Stroke symptoms

## 8. Follow-up

- Annual screening.
- Weight/waist.
- BP/sugar/lipids.
