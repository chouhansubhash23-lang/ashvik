# Injection Safety and Sharps Management

> **Source:** WHO injection safety; UIP; NHP
> **Evidence:** `../../evidence/sources.md`
> **Last verified:** 2026-10-28
> **Status:** Draft — clinician review required
> **Setting:** Rural OPD / PHC (India)

## 1. Quick facts

- Use **one needle, one syringe, one patient** — no exceptions.
- Never recap needles.
- Dispose sharps immediately in a puncture-proof container.
- Report needlestick injuries immediately.

## 2. Diagnosis / Assessment

- Review injection practices.
- Check sharps containers (fill level < 3/4).
- Check availability of gloves, alcohol swabs.
- Assess staff training.

## 3. Treatment: safe injection practice

| Step | Detail |
|---|---|
| 1 | Hand hygiene |
| 2 | Clean site with 70% alcohol |
| 3 | Use sterile needle/syringe |
| 4 | Do not recap |
| 5 | Dispose in sharps box immediately |
| 6 | Wash hands |

- Use **0.5 mL** vaccine dose as per antigen.
- Use safety-engineered devices where available.

## 4. Drug interactions and cautions

- **Dose:** not applicable (non-drug topic).
- **Paracetamol 500 mg** for post-injection pain/fever.
- **Anticoagulants:** pressure for longer after IM injection.
- **Needlestick:** HIV/HBV/HCV risk — follow PEP protocol.

## 5. Supportive care

- Immunise staff against hepatitis B.
- Provide PEP access.
- Train and audit.

## 6. Red flags

- Needlestick/sharp injury
- Blood-borne exposure
- Recapping/reuse observed
- Overfilled sharps container
- Unsafe disposal in general waste

## 7. Follow-up

- Report and document exposures.
- Follow PEP and serology.
- Audit injection practices.
