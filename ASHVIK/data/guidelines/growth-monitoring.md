# Growth Monitoring

> **Source:** WHO growth standards; POSHAN Abhiyaan; NHP
> **Evidence:** `../../evidence/sources.md`
> **Last verified:** 2026-10-28
> **Status:** Draft — clinician review required
> **Setting:** Rural OPD / PHC (India)

## 1. Quick facts

- Plot weight, length/height, and MUAC at every visit.
- Stunting (low height-for-age) and wasting (low weight-for-height) are different.
- Early detection prevents long-term consequences.
- Use WHO growth charts.

## 2. Diagnosis / Assessment

| Measure | Use |
|---|---|
| Weight-for-age | Underweight |
| Height/length-for-age | Stunting |
| Weight-for-height | Wasting |
| MUAC | SAM/MAM screening |
| Head circumference | Brain growth |

## 3. Classification

| Condition | Definition |
|---|---|
| Underweight | Weight-for-age < −2 SD |
| Stunting | Height-for-age < −2 SD |
| Wasting | Weight-for-height < −2 SD |
| SAM | < −3 SD or MUAC < 11.5 cm or oedema |
| MAM | −2 to −3 SD or MUAC 11.5–12.5 cm |

## 4. Treatment

| Finding | Action |
|---|---|
| Normal | Counselling, continue monitoring |
| MAM | Supplementary feeding, counselling |
| SAM | Refer to NRC; RUTF |
| Stunting | Nutrition, stimulation, follow-up |

- **Vitamin A 200,000 IU** (age ≥ 12 months) as per schedule.
- **Zinc 20 mg** for diarrhoea (14 days).
- **Albendazole 400 mg** deworming as per age.

## 5. Supportive care

- Promote breastfeeding and complementary feeding
- Growth chart at every visit
- Hand hygiene and sanitation
- Immunisation

## 6. Drug interactions and cautions

- **Vitamin A:** avoid overdose; not in pregnancy at high dose.
- **Zinc:** may cause vomiting; separate from iron.
- **Albendazole:** avoid in first trimester.
- **Iron:** constipation.

## 7. Red flags — refer immediately

- SAM with oedema/poor appetite
- No weight gain
- Severe anaemia
- Developmental delay
- Recurrent infections

## 8. Follow-up

- Monthly growth monitoring.
- Refer if no improvement.
- Nutrition counselling.
