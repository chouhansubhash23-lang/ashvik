# Oral Cancer Screening

> **Source:** National Oral Health Programme; ICMR; WHO
> **Evidence:** `../../evidence/sources.md`
> **Last verified:** 2026-10-28
> **Status:** Draft — clinician review required
> **Setting:** Rural OPD / PHC (India)

## 1. Quick facts

- Tobacco/betel quid is the major risk factor.
- Screen visually and palpate neck nodes.
- **Any non-healing ulcer > 2 weeks needs biopsy.**
- Early detection improves survival.

## 2. Clinical features

- Non-healing ulcer
- White/red patch
- Mass/lump
- Difficulty opening mouth
- Dysphagia/hoarseness
- Neck node

## 3. Diagnosis / Assessment

| Test | Use |
|---|---|
| Oral visual exam | Screening |
| Palpation | Neck nodes |
| Biopsy | Confirmation |
| Imaging | Staging (referral) |

## 4. Treatment

| Finding | Action |
|---|---|
| Suspicious lesion | Refer for biopsy |
| Tobacco use | Cessation counselling |
| Pain | Paracetamol 500 mg |
| Infection | Antibiotics |

**Brand examples (illustrative):** Dolo 650; Chlorhexidine — Hexidine.

## 5. Supportive care

- Tobacco cessation
- Oral hygiene
- Nutrition
- Psychological support

## 6. Drug interactions and cautions

- **Chlorhexidine:** staining; short-term.
- **NSAIDs:** bleeding.
- **Nicotine replacement:** follow guidance.
- **Biopsy:** do not delay.

## 7. Red flags — refer immediately

- Non-healing ulcer
- Indurated lesion
- Neck node
- Trismus
- Unexplained bleeding

## 8. Follow-up

- Biopsy result.
- Tobacco cessation.
- Annual screening.
