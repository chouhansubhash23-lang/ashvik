# Breastfeeding Support

> **Source:** WHO/UNICEF; RMNCH+A; NHP
> **Evidence:** `../../evidence/sources.md`
> **Last verified:** 2026-10-28
> **Status:** Draft — clinician review required
> **Setting:** Rural OPD / PHC (India)

## 1. Quick facts

- Exclusive breastfeeding for 6 months; continue to 2 years.
- Initiate within 1 hour of birth.
- Correct latch and position prevent problems.
- Support the mother; avoid unnecessary supplements.

## 2. Clinical features

- Poor latch, nipple pain
- Engorgement
- Low milk supply (perceived)
- Mastitis
- Infant poor weight gain

## 3. Diagnosis / Assessment

| Assessment | Detail |
|---|---|
| Latch | Mouth wide, lips flanged |
| Position | Baby's body aligned |
| Sucking | Slow deep sucks |
| Weight | Growth |
| Breast | Engorgement/cracks |

## 4. Treatment

| Problem | Management |
|---|---|
| Poor latch | Reposition, lactation support |
| Engorgement | Frequent feeding, warm compress |
| Mastitis | Continue feeding, antibiotics |
| Low supply | Frequent feeding, reassurance |
| Paracetamol 500 mg | For pain/fever |

**Brand examples (illustrative):** Augmentin; Dolo 650.

## 5. Supportive care

- Skin-to-skin
- Rooming-in
- Counselling
- Nutrition/hydration

## 6. Drug interactions and cautions

- **Antibiotics:** check compatibility with breastfeeding.
- **Sedatives:** avoid.
- **Supplements:** avoid unnecessary formula.
- **Paracetamol:** safe.

## 7. Red flags — refer immediately

- Infant not feeding
- Weight loss > 10%
- Fever/mastitis with systemic signs
- Dehydration
- Jaundice

## 8. Follow-up

- Weight check.
- Lactation counselling.
- Immunisation.
