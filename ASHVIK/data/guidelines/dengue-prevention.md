# Dengue Prevention

> **Source:** NVBDCP; WHO; NHP
> **Evidence:** `../../evidence/sources.md`
> **Last verified:** 2026-10-28
> **Status:** Draft — clinician review required
> **Setting:** Rural OPD / PHC (India)

## 1. Quick facts

- *Aedes aegypti* breeds in clean stagnant water.
- Eliminate breeding sites weekly.
- Use repellents, nets, full sleeves.
- Community participation is essential.

## 2. Clinical features (surveillance)

- Fever with rash/myalgias
- Warning signs
- Clusters in a locality

## 3. Diagnosis / Assessment

| Activity | Detail |
|---|---|
| Larval survey | Breeding sites |
| Fever surveillance | Early cases |
| NS1/IgM testing | Confirm |
| Reporting | Health authority |

## 4. Treatment / prevention

| Measure | Detail |
|---|---|
| Source reduction | Empty/cover containers |
| Larvicide | Temephos (as per programme) |
| Repellent | DEET 10–30% |
| Nets | Day sleeping |
| Paracetamol 500 mg | For fever (not prevention) |

**Brand examples (illustrative):** Dolo 650; Odomos.

## 5. Supportive care

- Health education
- Community clean-up
- School programmes

## 6. Drug interactions and cautions

- **Repellents:** avoid eyes/infants.
- **Larvicides:** follow dosing.
- **NSAIDs:** avoid in suspected dengue.
- **Vaccine:** as per national policy.

## 7. Red flags — refer immediately

- Warning signs in fever
- Cluster/outbreak
- Severe dengue

## 8. Follow-up

- Weekly larval survey.
- Case reporting.
- Outbreak response.
