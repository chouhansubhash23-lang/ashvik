# Contact Tracing

> **Source:** NCDC; WHO; NHP
> **Evidence:** `../../evidence/sources.md`
> **Last verified:** 2026-10-28
> **Status:** Draft — clinician review required
> **Setting:** Rural OPD / PHC (India)

## 1. Quick facts

- Identify and follow contacts of infectious cases.
- Break transmission chains.
- Confidentiality is essential.
- Use for TB, measles, COVID-19, STI, etc.

## 2. Steps

1. Identify index case
2. Define contact
3. List contacts
4. Notify and counsel
5. Test/quarantine as indicated
6. Follow up

## 3. Diagnosis / Assessment

| Activity | Detail |
|---|---|
| Exposure window | Infectious period |
| Contact definition | Close/prolonged |
| Testing | As per disease |
| Symptom monitoring | Daily |

## 4. Treatment / prophylaxis

| Disease | Prophylaxis |
|---|---|
| TB | TPT (6H/3HP) |
| Measles | Vaccine |
| HIV | PEP |
| Meningococcal | Chemoprophylaxis |
| Paracetamol 500 mg | Symptomatic |

**Brand examples (illustrative):** Isoniazid; Dolo 650.

## 5. Supportive care

- Counselling
- Stigma reduction
- Social support

## 6. Drug interactions and cautions

- **Confidentiality:** protect identity.
- **Prophylaxis:** follow guidelines.
- **Testing:** consent.
- **Isolation:** legal/ethical.

## 7. Red flags — escalate

- Large cluster
- High-risk contacts
- Non-cooperation
- Healthcare worker exposure

## 8. Follow-up

- Complete follow-up period.
- Document.
- Evaluate.
