# Adolescent Health

> **Source:** RKSK / MoHFW; WHO; NHP
> **Evidence:** `../../evidence/sources.md`
> **Last verified:** 2026-10-28

> **Setting:** Rural OPD / PHC (India)

## 1. Quick facts

- Adolescence (10–19 years) is a window for lifelong health.
- Key issues: nutrition, anaemia, mental health, reproductive health, substance use.
- Provide confidential, non-judgmental care.
- Link to RKSK adolescent-friendly clinics.

## 2. Common issues

| Domain | Issues |
|---|---|
| Nutrition | Anaemia, undernutrition, obesity |
| Mental health | Depression, anxiety, self-harm |
| Reproductive | Menstrual problems, contraception, STI |
| Substance use | Tobacco, alcohol, drugs |
| Injuries | Road traffic, self-harm |
| Screen time | Obesity, sleep, mental health |

## 3. Diagnosis / Assessment

- Growth chart, BMI, Hb
- Menstrual history
- Mental health screen (PHQ-9/GAD-7)
- Substance use history
- Confidential risk assessment

## 4. Treatment

| Issue | Action |
|---|---|
| Anaemia | Iron-folic acid (60 mg elemental iron + 500 mcg folic acid) daily |
| Menstrual problems | NSAID/OCP; refer if severe |
| Mental health | Counselling; SSRI if indicated |
| Contraception | Condoms/OCP; emergency contraception |
| Substance use | Brief intervention, de-addiction referral |

- **IFA 60 mg** daily for 6 months for anaemia.
- **Paracetamol 500 mg** for dysmenorrhoea.
- **Iron:** take with vitamin C; avoid tea.

## 5. Supportive care

- Confidential counselling
- Family involvement with consent
- School health linkage
- Nutrition and hygiene education

## 6. Drug interactions and cautions

- **OCPs:** contraindicated in migraine with aura, VTE.
- **SSRIs:** serotonin syndrome; monitor.
- **Iron:** constipation; separate from calcium.
- **NSAIDs:** GI upset.

## 7. Red flags — refer immediately

- Suicidal ideation/self-harm
- Severe anaemia
- Suspected sexual abuse
- Psychosis
- Severe malnutrition
- Substance withdrawal

## 8. Follow-up

- Regular review.
- Mental health follow-up.
- Contraception counselling.
