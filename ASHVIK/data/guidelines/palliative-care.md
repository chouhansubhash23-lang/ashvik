# Palliative Care and Pain Management

> **Source:** ICMR STW Vol. 3 (2022); National Palliative Care Programme; WHO
> **Evidence:** `../../evidence/sources.md`
> **Last verified:** 2026-10-27
> **Status:** Draft — clinician review required
> **Setting:** Rural OPD / PHC (India)

## 1. Quick facts

- Palliative care improves quality of life for serious illness — not only end-of-life care.
- Pain, breathlessness, nausea, constipation, and anxiety are the main symptoms.
- **Oral morphine** is safe and effective for severe cancer pain when used correctly.
- Care for the family/caregiver too.

## 2. Diagnosis / Assessment

| Symptom | Assess |
|---|---|
| Pain | Site, severity (0–10), character, triggers, current analgesia |
| Breathlessness | SpO2, effort, anxiety |
| Nausea/vomiting | Cause, frequency |
| Constipation | Opioid use, diet, hydration |
| Mood | Depression/anxiety |
| Function | ADL, mobility, caregiver support |

## 3. Treatment: pain management (WHO ladder)

| Step | Pain | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Mild | Paracetamol, NSAID |
| 2 | Moderate | Weak opioid (tramadol) ± paracetamol |
| 3 | Severe | Strong opioid (morphine) ± adjuvants |

### Oral morphine

| Aspect | Detail |
|---|---|
| Starting dose | 5–10 mg every 4 h (immediate release) or 15–30 mg sustained release BD |
| Titration | Increase by 30–50% if pain persists |
| Breakthrough | 10–15% of total daily dose as needed |
| Laxative | Always co-prescribe (senna/lactulose) |
| Side effects | Sedation, nausea, constipation, respiratory depression (rare with titration) |

**Brand examples (illustrative):** Morphine — Morphine sulfate; Senna — Senokot; Lactulose — Duphalac.

### Adjuvant analgesics

- Neuropathic pain: amitriptyline 10–25 mg HS, gabapentin 300 mg HS titrate.
- Bone pain: NSAID + radiotherapy referral.
- Muscle spasm: baclofen/diazepam (short-term).

## 4. Symptom control

| Symptom | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Breathlessness | Oxygen if hypoxic; morphine low dose; fan; reassurance |
| Nausea | Ondansetron 4–8 mg, metoclopramide 10 mg |
| Constipation | Senna + lactulose; fluids/fibre |
| Anxiety | Counselling; lorazepam short-term |
| Dry mouth | Oral care, sips, artificial saliva |
| Pressure ulcers | Repositioning, dressings |

## 5. Supportive care

- Advance care planning and goals of care
- Family meetings and caregiver support
- Spiritual/cultural needs
- Bereavement support
- Home-based palliative care linkage

## 6. Drug interactions and cautions

- **Morphine:** constipation (always laxative), nausea, sedation; reduce dose in renal failure; respiratory depression with sedatives.
- **Tramadol:** seizures, serotonin syndrome with SSRIs; avoid in epilepsy.
- **NSAIDs:** GI bleed, kidney injury; use with PPI in high risk.
- **Amitriptyline:** anticholinergic, arrhythmia; caution in elderly.
- **Gabapentin:** sedation, renal dose adjustment.
- **Ondansetron:** QT prolongation.

## 7. Red flags — refer immediately

- Uncontrolled severe pain
- Severe breathlessness at rest
- Massive bleeding
- Spinal cord compression (back pain + weakness/incontinence)
- Hypercalcaemia (confusion, constipation, dehydration)
- Suicidal ideation
- Caregiver crisis

## 8. Follow-up

- Regular home/OPD review.
- Reassess pain and side effects.
- Adjust opioids carefully.
- Support caregiver; link to community palliative services.
