# Drowning

> **Source:** WHO; ICMR; NHP
> **Evidence:** `../../evidence/sources.md`
> **Last verified:** 2026-10-28
> **Status:** Draft — clinician review required
> **Setting:** Rural OPD / PHC (India)

## 1. Quick facts

- Drowning = respiratory impairment from submersion.
- **Start CPR with rescue breaths** if unresponsive.
- Do not attempt to drain water from lungs.
- Assume spine injury in diving.

## 2. Clinical features

- Cough, breathlessness
- Hypoxia/cyanosis
- Altered consciousness
- Hypothermia
- Cardiac arrest

## 3. Diagnosis / Assessment

| Test | Use |
|---|---|
| SpO2 | Hypoxia |
| Consciousness | Neuro injury |
| Temperature | Hypothermia |
| Chest X-ray | Aspiration |

## 4. Treatment

| Step | Detail |
|---|---|
| 1 | Safe rescue |
| 2 | Check breathing/pulse |
| 3 | 5 rescue breaths, then CPR |
| 4 | Oxygen |
| 5 | Cervical spine immobilisation if trauma |
| 6 | Refer |

**Paracetamol 500 mg** for associated pain.

**Brand examples (illustrative):** Dolo 650.

## 5. Supportive care

- Warm
- Monitor 24 h (secondary drowning)
- Treat aspiration pneumonia

## 6. Drug interactions and cautions

- **Oxygen:** essential.
- **Sedatives:** avoid.
- **Diuretics:** not routine.
- **Antibiotics:** only if infection.

## 7. Red flags — refer immediately

- Cardiac arrest
- Hypoxia
- Altered consciousness
- Hypothermia
- Suspected spine injury

## 8. Follow-up

- Observe 24 h.
- Chest X-ray if symptoms.
- Prevention counselling.
