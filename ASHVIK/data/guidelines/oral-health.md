# Oral Health (Caries, Periodontal Disease, Oral Cancer Screening)

> **Source:** National Oral Health Programme; ICMR; WHO
> **Evidence:** `../../evidence/sources.md`
> **Last verified:** 2026-10-27
> **Status:** Draft — clinician review required
> **Setting:** Rural OPD / PHC (India)

## 1. Quick facts

- Dental caries and periodontal disease are the most common oral diseases.
- Tobacco/betel quid is a major cause of oral cancer in India.
- **Any non-healing oral ulcer > 2 weeks needs biopsy/referral.**
- Oral health is linked to diabetes and cardiovascular disease.

## 2. Clinical features

| Condition | Features |
|---|---|
| Dental caries | Tooth pain, cavity, sensitivity |
| Gingivitis | Bleeding gums, redness |
| Periodontitis | Loose teeth, bad breath, gum recession |
| Oral candidiasis | White patches, burning |
| Oral ulcer | Painful, usually heals in 7–10 days |
| Oral cancer | Non-healing ulcer, induration, white/red patch, mass |
| Dental abscess | Swelling, fever, trismus |

## 3. Diagnosis

| Test | Use |
|---|---|
| Clinical oral exam | All patients |
| X-ray (OPG) | Caries, impacted teeth |
| Biopsy | Suspicious ulcer/patch |
| Blood sugar | Diabetes screening |

## 4. Treatment

### 4.1 Dental caries / abscess

- Analgesia (paracetamol 500 mg three times daily or ibuprofen 400 mg three times daily)
- Dental referral for filling/extraction
- Antibiotics only for spreading infection (amoxicillin-clavulanate)

### 4.2 Gingivitis / periodontitis

- Oral hygiene instruction, scaling by dentist
- Chlorhexidine 0.2% mouthwash (short-term)
- Smoking cessation

### 4.3 Oral ulcers / candidiasis

| Condition | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Aphthous ulcer | Topical anaesthetic/steroid; vitamin B12 if deficient |
| Oral candidiasis | Nystatin suspension or clotrimazole troches x 7–14 days |
| Herpes labialis | Acyclovir cream |

**Brand examples (illustrative):** Chlorhexidine — Hexidine; Nystatin — Mycostatin; Acyclovir — Zovirax.

### 4.4 Oral cancer screening

- Visual inspection of oral cavity, tongue, floor of mouth.
- Palpate neck nodes.
- Refer for biopsy if suspicious.

## 5. Supportive care

- Brush twice daily with fluoride toothpaste
- Reduce sugar intake
- Stop tobacco/betel quid
- Regular dental check-ups
- Diabetes control

## 6. Drug interactions and cautions

- **Chlorhexidine:** staining, taste change; avoid with toothpaste (separate).
- **Nystatin:** topical; no major interactions.
- **Amoxicillin-clavulanate:** diarrhoea, cholestatic jaundice.
- **NSAIDs:** GI bleed, kidney injury.
- **Tobacco cessation:** offer counselling and nicotine replacement.

## 7. Red flags — refer immediately

- Non-healing ulcer > 2 weeks
- Suspicious white/red patch or mass
- Difficulty opening mouth/trismus
- Dysphagia/hoarseness
- Neck node
- Bleeding oral mass
- Dental abscess with facial swelling/fever

## 8. Follow-up

- Dental review as needed.
- Tobacco cessation follow-up.
- Biopsy follow-up for suspicious lesions.
