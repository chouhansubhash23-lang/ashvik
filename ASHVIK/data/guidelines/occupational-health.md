# Occupational Health (Silicosis, Pesticides, Heat, Noise)

> **Source:** ICMR/NCDC occupational health guidance; Ministry of Labour; WHO
> **Evidence:** `../../evidence/sources.md`
> **Last verified:** 2026-10-27
> **Status:** Draft — clinician review required
> **Setting:** Rural OPD / PHC (India)

## 1. Quick facts

- Rural workers face silica dust, pesticides, heat, noise, and ergonomic strain.
- **Silicosis** is irreversible; prevention is key.
- Pesticide exposure can be acute or chronic.
- Ask about occupation in every respiratory, skin, and neuro case.

## 2. Common occupational conditions

| Exposure | Condition |
|---|---|
| Silica dust (stone crushing, mining, construction) | Silicosis, TB risk |
| Cotton/grain dust | Byssinosis, asthma |
| Pesticides | Acute poisoning, neuropathy, dermatitis |
| Heat | Heat stroke, exhaustion |
| Noise | Hearing loss |
| Repetitive work | Musculoskeletal disorders |
| Solvents | Dermatitis, neurotoxicity |

## 3. Clinical features

- Silicosis: progressive breathlessness, cough, weight loss; often with TB
- Pesticide exposure: cholinergic symptoms, neuropathy, skin rash
- Heat illness: weakness, confusion, collapse
- Noise-induced hearing loss: gradual, bilateral
- Musculoskeletal: back/neck pain, wrist pain

## 4. Diagnosis

| Test | Use |
|---|---|
| Occupational history | Essential |
| Chest X-ray (ILO classification) | Silicosis |
| Spirometry | Restrictive/obstructive pattern |
| Sputum for TB | Silico-tuberculosis |
| Audiometry | Noise-induced hearing loss |
| Cholinesterase | Pesticide exposure |

## 5. Treatment and prevention

### 5.1 Silicosis

- No specific cure; stop exposure.
- Treat complications (TB, COPD, cor pulmonale).
- Vaccinate (influenza, pneumococcal).
- Refer for disability assessment/compensation.

### 5.2 Pesticide exposure

- Remove from exposure; wash skin; remove clothes.
- Acute poisoning: see poisoning chapter (atropine/pralidoxime).
- Chronic: neurological assessment; report.

### 5.3 Heat illness

- Move to cool place; cool rapidly; oral/IV fluids.
- Heat stroke: emergency cooling and referral.

### 5.4 Noise-induced hearing loss

- Hearing protection; audiometry.
- Refer for hearing aids.

### 5.5 Musculoskeletal

- Ergonomic advice, rest, physiotherapy, analgesia (paracetamol 500 mg three times daily).

## 6. Supportive care

- Workplace safety education
- Personal protective equipment (masks, gloves, ear plugs)
- Dust control, ventilation
- Regular health screening
- Compensation/legal support (ESIC, labour department)

## 7. Drug interactions and cautions

- **Analgesics:** avoid NSAIDs in kidney disease.
- **Steroids:** caution in silicosis/TB.
- **Pesticide antidotes:** see poisoning chapter.
- **Smoking:** synergistic with silica dust — counsel cessation.

## 8. Red flags — refer immediately

- Breathlessness with silica exposure
- Haemoptysis / suspected TB
- Acute pesticide poisoning
- Heat stroke
- Sudden hearing loss
- Severe musculoskeletal injury

## 9. Follow-up

- Annual spirometry/chest X-ray for silica-exposed workers.
- Audiometry for noise-exposed workers.
- TB screening in silicosis.
- Compensation documentation.
