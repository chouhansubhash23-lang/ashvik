# Nasogastric Tube and Urinary Catheter

> **Source:** ICMR STW Vol. 3 (2022); WHO; NHP
> **Evidence:** `../../evidence/sources.md`
> **Last verified:** 2026-10-28
> **Status:** Draft — clinician review required
> **Setting:** Rural OPD / PHC (India)

## 1. Quick facts

- NG tube: decompression/feeding in obstruction, ileus, pancreatitis, stroke.
- Urinary catheter: monitoring output, retention, obstruction.
- **Aseptic technique** prevents infection.
- Remove as soon as no longer needed.

## 2. Indications

| Tube | Indication |
|---|---|
| NG | Bowel obstruction, ileus, severe pancreatitis, vomiting, feeding |
| Urinary catheter | Retention, monitoring urine output, surgery, obstruction |

## 3. Diagnosis / Assessment

- Need for decompression/monitoring
- Contraindications: base of skull fracture (NG), urethral injury (catheter)
- Coagulation status
- Patient consent

## 4. Treatment: procedure

### NG tube

| Step | Detail |
|---|---|
| 1 | Measure length (nose–ear–xiphoid) |
| 2 | Lubricate and insert |
| 3 | Confirm position (aspirate pH, X-ray) |
| 4 | Secure and document |

### Urinary catheter

| Step | Detail |
|---|---|
| 1 | Aseptic technique, gloves |
| 2 | Clean urethral meatus |
| 3 | Lubricate, insert until urine flows |
| 4 | Inflate balloon (5–10 mL sterile water) |
| 5 | Connect drainage bag below bladder |

**Brand examples (illustrative):** Foley catheter; Ryle's tube.

## 5. Supportive care

- NG: oral care, check position before feeds, flush.
- Catheter: daily meatal care, keep bag below bladder, early removal.
- Monitor output and tolerance.

## 6. Drug interactions and cautions

- **NG feeds:** separate from medications (especially phenytoin, rifampicin, levothyroxine).
- **Catheter:** avoid unnecessary antibiotics for asymptomatic bacteriuria.
- **Sedation:** may be needed in agitated patients (caution).

## 7. Red flags — refer immediately

- NG misplacement (cough, breathlessness)
- GI bleeding
- Urethral trauma/bleeding
- Suspected urethral injury
- Sepsis from catheter
- Obstruction not relieved

## 8. Follow-up

- Review need daily.
- Remove early.
- Monitor for infection.
