# Elder Abuse Screening

> **Source:** WHO; NPHCE; NHP
> **Evidence:** `../../evidence/sources.md`
> **Last verified:** 2026-10-28
> **Status:** Draft — clinician review required
> **Setting:** Rural OPD / PHC (India)

## 1. Quick facts

- Elder abuse can be physical, emotional, financial, sexual, or neglect.
- Often hidden by fear/shame.
- Screen privately and sensitively.
- Report as per law; ensure safety.

## 2. Clinical features

- Unexplained injuries
- Poor hygiene/malnutrition
- Dehydration
- Fear of caregiver
- Delay in seeking care
- Financial exploitation
- Over-sedation

## 3. Diagnosis / Assessment

| Domain | Screen |
|---|---|
| Physical | Injuries, pressure ulcers |
| Emotional | Threats, isolation |
| Financial | Missing money/property |
| Neglect | Hygiene, food, medicines |
| Sexual | Unexplained genital injuries |

- Interview patient alone.
- Document findings objectively.

## 4. Treatment

| Situation | Action |
|---|---|
| Immediate danger | Safety plan, police/helpline |
| Medical needs | Treat injuries |
| Neglect | Social services |
| Financial | Legal aid |
| Emotional | Counselling |

**Paracetamol 500 mg** for pain; treat injuries.

**Brand examples (illustrative):** Paracetamol — Dolo 650.

## 5. Supportive care

- Confidentiality
- Counselling
- Shelter/legal support
- Helpline (Elder Line 14567)

## 6. Drug interactions and cautions

- **Sedatives:** may be used abusively — review.
- **Analgesics:** treat pain.
- **Documentation:** objective, no judgement.
- **Reporting:** follow legal requirements.

## 7. Red flags — refer immediately

- Serious injury
- Sexual abuse
- Neglect with dehydration/malnutrition
- Immediate danger
- Cognitive impairment with abuse

## 8. Follow-up

- Safety check.
- Social/legal support.
- Reassess.
