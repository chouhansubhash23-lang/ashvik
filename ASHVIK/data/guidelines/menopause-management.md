# Menopause Management

> **Source:** ICMR STW Vol. 3 (2022); NHP; WHO
> **Evidence:** `../../evidence/sources.md`
> **Last verified:** 2026-10-28
> **Status:** Draft — clinician review required
> **Setting:** Rural OPD / PHC (India)

## 1. Quick facts

- Menopause = 12 months of amenorrhoea after the last menstrual period.
- Symptoms: hot flushes, night sweats, mood changes, vaginal dryness.
- HRT is effective but needs individual risk assessment.
- Screen for osteoporosis and cardiovascular risk.

## 2. Clinical features

- Hot flushes/night sweats
- Sleep disturbance, irritability
- Vaginal dryness, dyspareunia
- Urinary symptoms
- Weight gain, joint pain

## 3. Diagnosis / Assessment

| Test | Use |
|---|---|
| FSH | Confirms menopause (if needed) |
| TSH | Thyroid dysfunction |
| CBC | Anaemia |
| Bone density | Osteoporosis risk |
| BP, lipids, glucose | Cardiovascular risk |
| Pelvic ultrasound | Bleeding |

## 4. Treatment

| Symptom | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Hot flushes | Lifestyle, HRT (if suitable) |
| Vaginal dryness | Vaginal moisturiser/estrogen |
| Osteoporosis | Calcium 500 mg BD + vitamin D 800 IU; bisphosphonate |
| Mood | Counselling; SSRI if needed |

### HRT

- Estrogen ± progestogen for women with uterus.
- Lowest effective dose, shortest duration.
- Contraindicated in breast cancer, VTE, liver disease, unexplained bleeding.

**Brand examples (illustrative):** Conjugated estrogen; Medroxyprogesterone; Calcium — Shelcal; Vitamin D — Uprise D3.

## 5. Supportive care

- Lifestyle: diet, exercise, weight, smoking cessation
- Vaginal lubricants
- Mental health support
- Bone health

## 6. Drug interactions and cautions

- **HRT:** VTE, stroke, breast cancer risk; individualise.
- **Calcium:** constipation; separate from iron.
- **Bisphosphonates:** esophageal irritation; take upright with water.
- **SSRIs:** hyponatraemia, bleeding risk.

## 7. Red flags — refer immediately

- Postmenopausal bleeding
- Breast lump
- Suspected VTE
- Severe osteoporosis/fracture
- Cardiovascular symptoms

## 8. Follow-up

- Annual review.
- Bone density as indicated.
- Cervical/breast screening.
