# COVID-19 and Influenza

> **Source:** ICMR COVID-19 guidance; WHO; NCDC influenza guidance
> **Evidence:** `../../evidence/sources.md`
> **Last verified:** 2026-10-27
> **Status:** Draft — clinician review required
> **Setting:** Rural OPD / PHC (India)

## 1. Quick facts

- Both are viral respiratory infections spread by droplets/aerosols.
- Most cases are mild; identify high-risk patients early.
- **Do not use antibiotics** unless bacterial co-infection is suspected.
- Oxygen and referral for severe disease save lives.

## 2. Clinical features

| Feature | COVID-19 | Influenza |
|---|---|---|
| Fever | Common | Common, sudden |
| Cough | Dry, common | Common |
| Sore throat | Common | Common |
| Body ache | Common | Severe |
| Loss of smell/taste | Possible | Rare |
| Breathlessness | In severe cases | In severe cases |
| GI symptoms | Possible | Less common |

## 3. Diagnosis

| Test | Use |
|---|---|
| COVID-19 RAT/RT-PCR | Confirm |
| Influenza RAT/PCR | Confirm |
| SpO2 | Severity |
| CBC, CRP | Inflammation |
| Chest X-ray | Pneumonia |

## 4. Treatment

### 4.1 Mild COVID-19 / influenza (home care)

| Measure | Detail |
|---|---|
| Isolation | Until fever-free and symptoms improving |
| Paracetamol | 500–650 mg every 6–8 h (max 4 g/day) |
| Hydration | Oral fluids |
| Monitoring | SpO2 twice daily; warning signs |
| Antivirals | High-risk COVID: nirmatrelvir/ritonavir within 5 days (specialist); influenza: oseltamivir within 48 h |

### 4.2 Influenza antiviral

| Drug | Brand example (illustrative) | Dose | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| Oseltamivir | Tamiflu | 75 mg twice daily (adult) | 5 days |
| Oseltamivir (children) | Tamiflu | Weight-based | 5 days |

### 4.3 Severe disease

- Oxygen to keep SpO2 ≥ 94% (COPD: 88–92%).
- Dexamethasone 6 mg daily x 10 days for severe COVID with hypoxia.
- Anticoagulation as per protocol.
- **Refer immediately** if SpO2 < 94%, breathlessness, or confusion.

**Brand examples:** Paracetamol — Dolo 650; Dexamethasone — Decadron.

## 5. Supportive care

- Rest, fluids, nutrition
- Prone positioning for hypoxaemia (awake proning)
- Monitor high-risk groups: elderly, diabetes, hypertension, obesity, pregnancy, immunocompromised
- Vaccination for prevention

## 6. Drug interactions and cautions

- **Nirmatrelvir/ritonavir:** potent CYP3A4 inhibitor — interacts with statins, calcium channel blockers, anticoagulants, anticonvulsants, immunosuppressants; check before use.
- **Oseltamivir:** nausea/vomiting; renal dose adjustment; generally safe in pregnancy.
- **Dexamethasone:** hyperglycaemia, infection, mood change; avoid in mild disease.
- **Antibiotics:** not routine; increase resistance.
- **NSAIDs:** avoid in dehydration/kidney injury.

## 7. Red flags — refer immediately

- SpO2 < 94% (or < 90% in COPD)
- Breathlessness at rest
- Chest pain, confusion
- Persistent high fever > 5 days
- Cyanosis
- Pregnancy with hypoxia
- Children with grunting, chest indrawing, inability to feed

## 8. Follow-up

- Review in 48–72 h if high risk.
- Post-COVID: assess for fatigue, breathlessness, anxiety.
- Vaccination and infection control counselling.
