# AEFI Reporting (Adverse Events Following Immunisation)

> **Source:** UIP AEFI surveillance; WHO; NHP
> **Evidence:** `../../evidence/sources.md`
> **Last verified:** 2026-10-28
> **Status:** Draft — clinician review required
> **Setting:** Rural OPD / PHC (India)

## 1. Quick facts

- Report **all** serious AEFI immediately.
- AEFI can be vaccine product, immunisation error, anxiety, or coincidental.
- Keep the vaccine vial and documents.
- Treat the patient first.

## 2. Diagnosis / Assessment

| Type | Example |
|---|---|
| Minor | Local pain, mild fever |
| Serious | Anaphylaxis, seizure, hospitalization |
| Cluster | Multiple cases same vaccine/lot |

## 3. Treatment: immediate management

| Reaction | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Anaphylaxis | Adrenaline 0.01 mg/kg IM (max 0.5 mg), oxygen, fluids |
| Fever | Paracetamol 15 mg/kg/dose |
| Seizure | Protect airway; lorazepam if prolonged |
| Abscess | Drain, antibiotics |

- Keep **adrenaline 0.5 mg** ready.
- Refer serious cases.

## 4. Reporting

- Fill AEFI form.
- Inform district immunisation officer within 24 h.
- Send vaccine vial/syringe if requested.
- Document lot number.

## 5. Supportive care

- Reassure parents.
- Explain reporting process.
- Follow up.

## 6. Drug interactions and cautions

- **Adrenaline:** tachycardia; do not delay in anaphylaxis.
- **Paracetamol:** max 4 doses/day.
- **Sedatives:** avoid unless needed.
- **Do not discard** vaccine vial.

## 7. Red flags — refer immediately

- Anaphylaxis
- Persistent seizure
- Encephalopathy
- Hospitalization
- Cluster of cases

## 8. Follow-up

- Track outcome.
- Report final diagnosis.
- Audit immunisation practices.
