# Intestinal Obstruction

> **Source:** ICMR STW Vol. 3 (2022); WHO; NHP
> **Evidence:** `../../evidence/sources.md`
> **Last verified:** 2026-10-27
> **Status:** Draft — clinician review required
> **Setting:** Rural OPD / PHC (India)

## 1. Quick facts

- Obstruction can be small bowel or large bowel; mechanical or paralytic ileus.
- Causes: adhesions, hernia, volvulus, intussusception, tumour, TB, gallstone ileus.
- **Do not give laxatives/enemas** in suspected obstruction.
- Refer for surgical evaluation.

## 2. Clinical features

- Colicky abdominal pain
- Vomiting (bilious/feculent)
- Distension
- Absolute constipation (no flatus/stool)
- High-pitched bowel sounds (early), absent (late)
- Dehydration, tachycardia
- Hernia at groin (obstructed)

## 3. Diagnosis

| Test | Use |
|---|---|
| Clinical exam | Distension, hernia orifices, bowel sounds |
| Erect/decubitus X-ray | Air-fluid levels, free gas |
| CBC, electrolytes, creatinine | Dehydration |
| Ultrasound | Intussusception, ascites |
| CT | If available, cause/site |

## 4. Treatment

| Measure | Detail |
|---|---|
| Nil by mouth | Yes |
| IV fluids | 0.9% saline/Ringer's lactate |
| NG tube | Decompression |
| Electrolytes | Correct K+, Na+ |
| Urinary catheter | Monitor output |
| Antibiotics | Ceftriaxone 1 g IV + metronidazole 500 mg IV if strangulation/perforation suspected |
| Surgery | Refer |

**Brand examples (illustrative):** Ceftriaxone — Monocef; Metronidazole — Flagyl.

## 5. Supportive care

- Monitor vitals, urine output, abdominal girth
- Keep warm
- Pain management
- Arrange transport

## 6. Drug interactions and cautions

- **Laxatives/enemas:** avoid — risk of perforation.
- **Opioids:** may mask; use judiciously.
- **NSAIDs:** avoid in dehydration/kidney injury.
- **Antiemetics:** may mask vomiting; use cautiously.

## 7. Red flags — refer immediately

- Strangulation (fever, tachycardia, tenderness, rigidity)
- Perforation (free gas, peritonitis)
- Shock
- Obstructed hernia
- Volvulus
- Pregnancy with obstruction
- No improvement with conservative care

## 8. Follow-up

- Surgical review.
- Post-op care.
- Investigate cause (adhesions, TB, tumour).
