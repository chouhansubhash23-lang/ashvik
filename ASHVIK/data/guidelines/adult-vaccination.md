# Adult Vaccination

> **Source:** UIP / MoHFW adult immunisation guidance; WHO
> **Evidence:** `../../evidence/sources.md`
> **Last verified:** 2026-10-28
> **Status:** Draft — clinician review required
> **Setting:** Rural OPD / PHC (India)

## 1. Quick facts

- Adults need vaccines based on age, comorbidity, pregnancy, and occupation.
- Check vaccination history and contraindications.
- Keep a vaccination card.
- Mild illness is not a contraindication.

## 2. Recommended adult vaccines

| Vaccine | Who | Schedule |
|---|---|---|
| Td/TT | All adults | Booster every 10 years |
| Influenza | Elderly, chronic disease, pregnancy, healthcare workers | Yearly |
| Pneumococcal | Elderly > 65, chronic lung/heart disease, diabetes | Once (as per guidance) |
| Hepatitis B | Healthcare workers, dialysis, high-risk | 0, 1, 6 months |
| HPV | Girls/adolescents (as per programme) | 2 doses |
| COVID-19 | As per national guidance | Boosters as advised |
| Rabies | Post-exposure | 0, 3, 7, 14, 28 days |
| Hepatitis A | High-risk/travel | 2 doses |
| Varicella | Non-immune high-risk | 2 doses |
| Shingles | Elderly (if available) | 2 doses |

## 3. Diagnosis / Assessment

- Vaccination history
- Allergy history (anaphylaxis to component)
- Pregnancy/immunosuppression
- Acute severe illness (defer)
- Current medications (steroids, chemotherapy)

## 4. Treatment: administration

| Step | Detail |
|---|---|
| 1 | Consent and screening |
| 2 | Check expiry/cold chain |
| 3 | Correct site (deltoid IM; SC for some) |
| 4 | Aseptic technique |
| 5 | Observe 15–30 min |
| 6 | Document and schedule next dose |

**Paracetamol 500 mg** for post-vaccine fever/aches.

## 5. Supportive care

- Cold compress for local pain
- Encourage completion of schedule
- Educate on AEFI

## 6. Drug interactions and cautions

- **Live vaccines:** avoid in pregnancy/immunosuppression.
- **Steroids/chemotherapy:** live vaccines contraindicated.
- **Anticoagulants:** IM injection caution.
- **Anaphylaxis history:** refer to allergy specialist.

## 7. Red flags — refer immediately

- Anaphylaxis
- Severe local reaction
- High fever > 3 days
- Neurological symptoms
- Pregnancy with live vaccine exposure

## 8. Follow-up

- Document in card/register.
- Remind for next dose.
- Report AEFI.
