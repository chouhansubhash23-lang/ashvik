# Paediatric Dosing Guide (Common Drugs)

> **Source:** IAP drug formulary; WHO; NHP
> **Evidence:** `../../evidence/sources.md`
> **Last verified:** 2026-10-28
> **Status:** Draft — clinician review required
> **Setting:** Rural OPD / PHC (India)

## 1. Quick facts

- Always dose by **weight (kg)**, not age alone.
- Check maximum adult dose.
- Adjust in renal/hepatic impairment.
- Use a dosing calculator/weight band chart.

## 2. Common drugs

| Drug | Dose | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Paracetamol | 15 mg/kg/dose | Every 6 h (max 4/day) |
| Ibuprofen | 10 mg/kg/dose | Every 8 h (≥ 6 months) |
| Amoxicillin | 40–45 mg/kg/day | Divided BD/TDS |
| Amoxicillin-clavulanate | 30–45 mg/kg/day | Divided BD |
| Azithromycin | 10 mg/kg/day | Once daily |
| Ceftriaxone | 75–100 mg/kg/day | Once daily (IV) |
| ORS | 50–100 mL after each stool | As needed |
| Zinc | 10 mg (< 6 months), 20 mg (≥ 6 months) | Daily x 14 days |
| Vitamin A | 100,000 IU (6–11 months), 200,000 IU (≥ 12 months) | As per schedule |
| Albendazole | 400 mg (≥ 2 years) | Single dose |
| Salbutamol | 2.5 mg neb or 2 puffs | As needed |

**Brand examples (illustrative):** Calpol; Brufen; Mox; Azee; Monocef.

## 3. Diagnosis / Assessment

- Weight and age
- Renal/hepatic function
- Allergies
- Current medications
- Maximum dose

## 4. Treatment principles

- Use weight-based dosing.
- Round to measurable volumes.
- Avoid adult tablets when pediatric formulations exist.
- Double-check high-risk drugs (digoxin, opioids, chemotherapy).

## 5. Supportive care

- Explain dosing to parents.
- Provide syringe/dropper.
- Write clear instructions.

## 6. Drug interactions and cautions

- **Paracetamol:** hepatotoxicity with overdose.
- **Ibuprofen:** avoid in dehydration/kidney injury.
- **Ceftriaxone:** avoid in neonates with calcium.
- **Aspirin:** contraindicated.
- **Codeine:** avoid in children.

## 7. Red flags

- Dose exceeds maximum
- Wrong formulation
- Renal impairment
- Neonate/preterm
- Suspected overdose

## 8. Follow-up

- Review response.
- Adjust dose with weight gain.
- Document.
