# School Health Screening

> **Source:** RBSK / School Health Programme; MoHFW; WHO
> **Evidence:** `../../evidence/sources.md`
> **Last verified:** 2026-10-28
> **Status:** Draft — clinician review required
> **Setting:** Rural OPD / PHC (India)

## 1. Quick facts

- Screening detects vision, hearing, anaemia, dental, and developmental problems.
- Early referral improves outcomes.
- Use RBSK/School Health formats.
- Maintain confidentiality.

## 2. Screening components

| Domain | Test |
|---|---|
| Vision | Snellen chart |
| Hearing | Whisper test/otoscopy |
| Anaemia | Hb/pallor |
| Nutrition | Height/weight/BMI |
| Dental | Oral exam |
| Skin | Scabies/fungal |
| Development | Milestones/learning |
| Immunisation | Card check |

## 3. Diagnosis / Assessment

- Record findings on health card.
- Identify red flags.
- Refer to appropriate level.

## 4. Treatment

| Finding | Action |
|---|---|
| Anaemia | Iron-folic acid 60 mg elemental iron + 500 mcg folic acid daily |
| Vision defect | Refer for glasses |
| Hearing loss | ENT referral |
| Dental caries | Dental referral |
| Scabies | Permethrin 5% cream |
| Malnutrition | Nutrition counselling/referral |

**Brand examples (illustrative):** Autrin; Permite; Calpol 500 mg.

## 5. Supportive care

- Health education
- Hand hygiene
- Nutrition
- Deworming

## 6. Drug interactions and cautions

- **Iron:** constipation; separate from calcium.
- **Permethrin:** avoid eyes.
- **Paracetamol 500 mg** for pain/fever.
- **Referral:** ensure follow-up.

## 7. Red flags — refer immediately

- Severe anaemia
- Vision loss
- Hearing loss
- Heart murmur
- Developmental delay
- Suspected abuse

## 8. Follow-up

- Track referrals.
- Re-screen annually.
- Update health cards.
