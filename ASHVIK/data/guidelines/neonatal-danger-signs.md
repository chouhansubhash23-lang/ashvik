# Neonatal Danger Signs

> **Source:** IMNCI / F-IMNCI; WHO; NHP
> **Evidence:** `../../evidence/sources.md`
> **Last verified:** 2026-10-28
> **Status:** Draft — clinician review required
> **Setting:** Rural OPD / PHC (India)

## 1. Quick facts

- Neonates deteriorate quickly — recognise danger signs early.
- Any danger sign = urgent referral.
- Keep the baby warm (skin-to-skin) during transport.
- Support breastfeeding and hygiene.

## 2. Danger signs

- Not feeding / poor suck
- Lethargy / unconscious
- Fast breathing (> 60/min) or grunting
- Chest indrawing
- Hypothermia (< 36.5 °C) or fever
- Convulsions
- Jaundice in first 24 h or palms/soles
- Abdominal distension / vomiting
- Umbilical redness/discharge
- Skin pustules
- Bleeding

## 3. Diagnosis / Assessment

| Check | Detail |
|---|---|
| Weight | Low birth weight |
| Temperature | Hypothermia/sepsis |
| Respiratory rate | Pneumonia |
| Feeding | Suck/swallow |
| Jaundice | Severity |
| Umbilicus/skin | Infection |

## 4. Treatment

| Measure | Detail |
|---|---|
| Keep warm | Skin-to-skin, wrap, cap |
| Feeding | Breastfeed/expressed milk |
| Glucose | Check/treat hypoglycaemia |
| Oxygen | If hypoxic |
| Antibiotics | Refer — ampicillin 50 mg/kg + gentamicin (facility) |
| Referral | Urgent |

**Brand examples (illustrative):** Ampicillin; Gentamicin.

## 5. Supportive care

- Hand hygiene
- Clean cord care
- Exclusive breastfeeding
- Kangaroo mother care
- Immunisation at birth

## 6. Drug interactions and cautions

- **Gentamicin:** nephro/ototoxic; dose by weight.
- **Ampicillin:** allergy; renal adjustment.
- **Paracetamol 10–15 mg/kg** for fever (not aspirin).
- **Avoid unnecessary antibiotics.**

## 7. Red flags — refer immediately

- Any danger sign
- Weight < 1.5 kg
- Severe jaundice
- Apnoea
- Seizures
- Suspected sepsis/meningitis

## 8. Follow-up

- Review 48–72 h after referral.
- Growth and feeding check.
- Immunisation schedule.
