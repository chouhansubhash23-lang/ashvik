# Developmental Delay

> **Source:** RBSK; WHO; NHP
> **Evidence:** `../../evidence/sources.md`
> **Last verified:** 2026-10-28
> **Status:** Draft — clinician review required
> **Setting:** Rural OPD / PHC (India)

## 1. Quick facts

- Early detection enables early intervention.
- Use milestone checklists at every visit.
- Delay can be global or specific (motor, language, social).
- Refer for assessment and therapy.

## 2. Clinical features

- Not sitting by 9 months
- Not walking by 18 months
- No words by 18 months
- No pointing/social smile
- Poor eye contact
- Regression of skills

## 3. Diagnosis / Assessment

| Domain | Milestones |
|---|---|
| Gross motor | Head control 3 m, sit 6–8 m, walk 12–18 m |
| Fine motor | Grasp, pincer at 9–12 m |
| Language | Babble 6 m, words 12–18 m, sentences 2–3 y |
| Social | Smile 6 w, stranger anxiety 6–9 m |

- Hearing and vision assessment.
- Thyroid function.
- Iron/lead if indicated.

## 4. Treatment

| Finding | Action |
|---|---|
| Delay | Refer for early intervention |
| Hearing loss | Audiology referral |
| Hypothyroidism | Levothyroxine 10–15 mcg/kg/day |
| Nutrition | Correct anaemia/malnutrition (iron 3 mg/kg/day) |
| Seizures | AED |

**Brand examples (illustrative):** Levothyroxine — Thyronorm; Iron — Autrin.

## 5. Supportive care

- Parent counselling
- Stimulation activities
- Nutrition
- Immunisation

## 6. Drug interactions and cautions

- **Levothyroxine:** separate from calcium/iron.
- **Iron:** constipation.
- **Sedatives:** avoid.
- **Vaccines:** complete schedule.

## 7. Red flags — refer immediately

- Regression of milestones
- Seizures
- Microcephaly
- Suspected hearing/vision loss
- Severe malnutrition
- Suspected autism

## 8. Follow-up

- Regular developmental assessment.
- Early intervention.
- School readiness.
