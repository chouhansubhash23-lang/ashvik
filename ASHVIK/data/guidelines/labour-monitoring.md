# Labour Monitoring

> **Source:** RMNCH+A / NHM; WHO; NHP
> **Evidence:** `../../evidence/sources.md`
> **Last verified:** 2026-10-28
> **Status:** Draft — clinician review required
> **Setting:** Rural OPD / PHC (India)

## 1. Quick facts

- Use a partograph to monitor progress.
- Assess maternal vitals, fetal heart, contractions, and dilation.
- Identify obstructed labour early.
- Refer if progress is abnormal.

## 2. Clinical features

- Regular painful contractions
- Cervical dilation
- Show/rupture of membranes
- Fetal heart rate
- Maternal pulse/BP

## 3. Diagnosis / Assessment

| Parameter | Frequency |
|---|---|
| Fetal heart | Every 30 min (1st stage) |
| Contractions | Every 30 min |
| Dilation | Every 4 h |
| BP/pulse | Every 4 h |
| Temperature | Every 4 h |

## 4. Treatment

| Issue | Action |
|---|---|
| Normal labour | Support, hydration |
| Poor progress | Assess cause; refer |
| Fetal distress | Position, oxygen, refer |
| Obstructed labour | Emergency referral |
| PPH | See PPH protocol |

**Paracetamol 500 mg** for pain (if appropriate).

**Brand examples (illustrative):** Dolo 650.

## 5. Supportive care

- Companion of choice
- Hydration
- Bladder care
- Pain relief

## 6. Drug interactions and cautions

- **Oxytocin:** only with monitoring (specialist).
- **Sedatives:** avoid (fetal depression).
- **Antibiotics:** for prolonged rupture.
- **Paracetamol:** safe.

## 7. Red flags — refer immediately

- Obstructed labour
- Fetal distress
- Bleeding
- Cord prolapse
- Eclampsia
- Sepsis

## 8. Follow-up

- Postpartum review.
- Newborn care.
- Documentation.
