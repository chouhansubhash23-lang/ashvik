# Palliative Symptom Protocols

> **Source:** ICMR STW Vol. 3 (2022); National Palliative Care Programme; WHO
> **Evidence:** `../../evidence/sources.md`
> **Last verified:** 2026-10-27
> **Status:** Draft — clinician review required
> **Setting:** Rural OPD / PHC (India)

## 1. Quick facts

- Palliative care addresses pain and other distressing symptoms.
- Use a stepwise, protocol-based approach.
- **Always co-prescribe a laxative with opioids.**
- Reassess frequently; involve the family.

## 2. Diagnosis / Assessment and pain protocol

| Step | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Mild | Paracetamol 500–650 mg TDS |
| Moderate | Tramadol 50–100 mg TDS ± paracetamol |
| Severe | Oral morphine 5–10 mg every 4 h; titrate |
| Breakthrough | 10–15% of total daily dose |
| Neuropathic | Amitriptyline 10–25 mg HS or gabapentin 300 mg HS |

**Always add a laxative** (senna ± lactulose) with opioids.

## 3. Treatment: dyspnoea protocol

- Oxygen if hypoxic.
- Low-dose morphine 2.5–5 mg orally (if opioid-naive) for refractory breathlessness.
- Fan/air movement; relax breathing.
- Treat reversible causes (effusion, anaemia, infection).
- Anxiolytic if panic component.

## 4. Nausea/vomiting protocol

| Cause | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Gastric stasis | Metoclopramide 10 mg TDS |
| Chemical/metabolic | Haloperidol 0.5–1 mg HS |
| Vestibular | Promethazine 12.5–25 mg |
| General | Ondansetron 4–8 mg TDS |

## 5. Constipation protocol

- Senna 1–2 tablets at night ± lactulose 15–30 mL daily.
- Encourage fluids/fibre if possible.
- Review opioids and other constipating drugs.

## 6. Respiratory secretions ("death rattle")

- Reposition; suction only if distressing.
- Hyoscine butylbromide 20 mg SC/IV; or glycopyrrolate.
- Explain to family that it is not painful.

## 7. Terminal restlessness / agitation

- Rule out pain, urinary retention, constipation, hypoxia, hypoglycaemia.
- Midazolam 2.5–5 mg SC/IV (specialist); or haloperidol.
- Ensure safety; involve family.

## 8. Drug interactions and cautions

- **Morphine:** constipation, nausea, sedation, respiratory depression; reduce dose in renal failure.
- **Tramadol:** seizures, serotonin syndrome.
- **Metoclopramide:** extrapyramidal symptoms.
- **Haloperidol:** QT prolongation, extrapyramidal.
- **Midazolam:** respiratory depression; monitor.

## 9. Red flags — refer immediately

- Uncontrolled severe pain
- Severe breathlessness at rest
- Massive bleeding
- Spinal cord compression
- Hypercalcaemia
- Seizures
- Caregiver crisis

## 10. Follow-up

- Daily review in terminal phase.
- Adjust doses to symptoms.
- Support caregiver and family.
- Document goals of care.
