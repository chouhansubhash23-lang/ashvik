# Chikungunya and Viral Fever

> **Source:** NVBDCP chikungunya guidance; NCDC; WHO
> **Evidence:** `../../evidence/sources.md`
> **Last verified:** 2026-10-27
> **Status:** Draft — clinician review required
> **Setting:** Rural OPD / PHC (India)

## 1. Quick facts

- **Chikungunya:** *Aedes*-borne alphavirus; fever with severe joint pain; outbreaks in monsoon/post-monsoon.
- **Viral fever:** broad category — dengue, chikungunya, influenza, viral hepatitis, etc. must be excluded.
- No specific antiviral for most viral fevers; supportive care.
- Distinguish from dengue and malaria before discharging.

## 2. Clinical features

| Feature | Chikungunya | Dengue |
|---|---|---|
| Fever | Sudden high | Sudden high |
| Joint pain | Severe, often disabling | Mild-moderate |
| Rash | Common | Common |
| Bleeding | Rare | Possible |
| Platelets | Usually normal | Often low |

- Chikungunya: joint pain may persist weeks to months.
- Viral fever: sore throat, cough, body ache, headache, fatigue.

## 3. Diagnosis

| Test | Use |
|---|---|
| Malaria RDT/smear | Exclude malaria |
| Dengue NS1/IgM | Exclude dengue |
| Chikungunya IgM | Confirm (after day 5) |
| CBC | Platelets, leucocytes |
| Typhoid test/culture | If fever > 1 week |

## 4. Treatment (supportive)

| Measure | Detail |
|---|---|
| Fever/body ache | Paracetamol 500–650 mg (adult) every 6–8 h; max 4 g/day |
| Hydration | Oral fluids/ORS |
| Joint pain | Paracetamol; avoid NSAIDs until dengue excluded |
| Rest | Advised |
| Antihistamine | Cetirizine 10 mg at night for rash/itching |

**Brand examples (illustrative):** Paracetamol — Dolo 650, Crocin; Cetirizine — Cetzine.

**Do not use aspirin/ibuprofen/diclofenac** until dengue is ruled out.

## 5. Supportive care

- Monitor for warning signs daily.
- Encourage fluids and rest.
- Physiotherapy for persistent joint pain.
- Isolate from mosquito bites (bed net) during febrile period.

## 6. Drug interactions and cautions

- **Paracetamol:** hepatotoxicity with alcohol/overdose; warfarin interaction; max 4 g/day.
- **NSAIDs:** bleeding risk in dengue; GI/kidney effects.
- **Cetirizine:** sedation; caution with alcohol.
- **Avoid steroids** for chikungunya joint pain unless specialist advice.

## 7. Red flags — refer immediately

- Bleeding, severe abdominal pain, persistent vomiting (dengue)
- Altered consciousness, seizures
- Breathlessness, chest pain
- Severe dehydration
- Pregnancy with fever
- Infants and elderly with poor intake
- Joint pain with fever > 10 days (consider other causes)

## 8. Follow-up

- Review in 48–72 hours.
- Repeat CBC if dengue suspected.
- Refer if joint pain persists > 6 weeks.
