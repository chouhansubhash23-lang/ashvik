# Wound Care Formulary

> **Source:** ICMR; WHO; NHP
> **Evidence:** `../../evidence/sources.md`
> **Last verified:** 2026-10-28
> **Status:** Draft — clinician review required
> **Setting:** Rural OPD / PHC (India)

## 1. Quick facts

- Choose dressing based on wound type and exudate.
- Moist wound healing is preferred.
- Clean with saline; avoid harsh antiseptics.
- Debride necrotic tissue.

## 2. Wound types

| Type | Dressing |
|---|---|
| Clean surgical | Simple dressing |
| Granulating | Hydrocolloid/foam |
| Exudative | Alginate/foam |
| Necrotic | Hydrogel + debridement |
| Infected | Antimicrobial dressing |
| Burns | Silver sulfadiazine/non-adherent |

## 3. Diagnosis / Assessment

- Size, depth, exudate
- Infection signs
- Perfusion
- Pain
- Surrounding skin

## 4. Treatment

| Product | Use |
|---|---|
| Saline | Cleaning |
| Hydrogel | Dry/necrotic wounds |
| Alginate | High exudate |
| Foam | Moderate exudate |
| Hydrocolloid | Low exudate |
| Silver sulfadiazine | Burns/infected |

- **Paracetamol 500 mg** for wound pain.
- **1% povidone-iodine** for surrounding skin (not wound bed).

**Brand examples (illustrative):** Silvadene; Duoderm; Kaltostat.

## 5. Supportive care

- Offloading
- Nutrition
- Infection control
- Pain management

## 6. Drug interactions and cautions

- **Antiseptics:** may impair healing.
- **Silver:** avoid in G6PD/pregnancy near term.
- **Antibiotics:** only for infection.
- **Adhesives:** skin allergy.

## 7. Red flags — refer immediately

- Spreading infection
- Necrotising fasciitis
- Exposed bone/tendon
- No healing after 2 weeks
- Severe pain

## 8. Follow-up

- Review dressing every 1–3 days.
- Reassess wound.
- Refer if not healing.
