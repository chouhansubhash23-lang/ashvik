# Infertility Referral

> **Source:** ICMR; WHO; NHP
> **Evidence:** `../../evidence/sources.md`
> **Last verified:** 2026-10-28
> **Status:** Draft — clinician review required
> **Setting:** Rural OPD / PHC (India)

## 1. Quick facts

- Infertility = no conception after 12 months of unprotected intercourse.
- Assess both partners.
- Refer early for age > 35, irregular cycles, or abnormal semen.
- Provide counselling and confidentiality.

## 2. Clinical features

- Irregular/absent periods
- Dysmenorrhoea
- Pelvic pain
- Male sexual dysfunction
- Varicocele

## 3. Diagnosis / Assessment

| Test | Partner | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Semen analysis | Male | First-line |
| Ovulation tracking | Female | Cycle |
| TSH/prolactin | Female | Endocrine |
| Pelvic ultrasound | Female | PCOS/fibroids |
| HSG | Female | Tubal patency |

## 4. Treatment

| Cause | Action |
|---|---|
| PCOS | Weight loss, letrozole 2.5–5 mg |
| Tubal block | IVF referral |
| Male factor | Andrology referral |
| Thyroid | Treat |
| Unexplained | Refer |

**Brand examples (illustrative):** Letroz — Letrozole; Glycomet; Folvite.

## 5. Supportive care

- Couple counselling
- Lifestyle
- Folic acid 5 mg
- Financial support

## 6. Drug interactions and cautions

- **Letrozole/clomiphene:** multiple pregnancy/OHSS.
- **Metformin:** GI upset.
- **Avoid unproven remedies.**
- **Paracetamol 500 mg** for pain.

## 7. Red flags — refer immediately

- Age > 35 with 6 months trying
- Abnormal semen
- Severe dysmenorrhoea
- History of PID/STI
- Recurrent pregnancy loss

## 8. Follow-up

- Review after 3 months.
- Refer if no conception.
- Counselling.
