# Childhood Obesity

> **Source:** ICMR; WHO; NHP
> **Evidence:** `../../evidence/sources.md`
> **Last verified:** 2026-10-28
> **Status:** Draft — clinician review required
> **Setting:** Rural OPD / PHC (India)

## 1. Quick facts

- Rising in urban and rural India.
- Use BMI-for-age percentile (WHO/IAP).
- Lifestyle change is first-line.
- Screen for comorbidities (BP, sugar, lipids).

## 2. Clinical features

- Weight gain, central adiposity
- Snoring, fatigue
- Acanthosis nigricans
- Joint pain
- Psychological impact

## 3. Diagnosis / Assessment

| Measure | Use |
|---|---|
| BMI-for-age | ≥ 95th percentile = obesity |
| Waist circumference | Central obesity |
| BP | Hypertension |
| Fasting glucose/HbA1c | Diabetes |
| Lipids | Dyslipidaemia |
| Thyroid | Hypothyroidism |

## 4. Treatment

| Measure | Detail |
|---|---|
| Diet | Reduce sugar/refined carbs; balanced meals |
| Activity | 60 min/day |
| Screen time | < 2 h/day |
| Sleep | 8–10 h |
| Family involvement | Essential |

- **No routine weight-loss drugs** in children.
- Treat comorbidities.

**Brand examples (illustrative):** Metformin 500 mg (if insulin resistance, specialist); Vitamin D 600 IU.

## 5. Supportive care

- Behavioural counselling
- School/community programmes
- Avoid stigma

## 6. Drug interactions and cautions

- **Metformin:** GI upset; specialist.
- **Stimulants:** avoid.
- **Weight-loss supplements:** unsafe.
- **Insulin:** if diabetes.

## 7. Red flags — refer immediately

- Severe obesity with sleep apnoea
- Hypertension
- Diabetes
- Pseudotumour cerebri
- Suspected endocrine cause

## 8. Follow-up

- Monthly weight/BMI.
- Comorbidity screening.
- Family counselling.

## 9. Sources

## 10. Disclaimer

Decision-support summary only. Childhood obesity needs family-based lifestyle intervention. Brand names are illustrative, not promotional.
