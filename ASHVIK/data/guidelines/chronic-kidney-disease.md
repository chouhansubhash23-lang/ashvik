# Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD)

> **Source:** ICMR STW Vol. 3 (2022); KDIGO; NPCDCS
> **Evidence:** `../../evidence/sources.md`
> **Last verified:** 2026-10-27
> **Status:** Draft — clinician review required
> **Setting:** Rural OPD / PHC (India)

## 1. Quick facts

- CKD = kidney damage or eGFR < 60 mL/min/1.73 m² for ≥ 3 months.
- Diabetes and hypertension are the leading causes in India.
- Early detection and BP/sugar control slow progression.
- Avoid nephrotoxins; adjust drug doses.

## 2. Clinical features

Often asymptomatic until advanced:
- Fatigue, weakness, poor appetite
- Swelling (legs, face)
- Breathlessness
- Nausea, vomiting, itching
- Nocturia, foamy urine
- Anaemia, hypertension

## 3. Diagnosis and staging

| Test | Use |
|---|---|
| Serum creatinine + eGFR | Stage CKD |
| Urine albumin/creatinine ratio | Kidney damage |
| Urine dipstick | Protein, blood |
| Ultrasound KUB | Small kidneys, obstruction |
| CBC, calcium, phosphate, PTH | Complications |
| HbA1c, lipids | Risk factors |

| Stage | eGFR | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | ≥ 60 with damage | Control BP/sugar; yearly review |
| 3a | 45–59 | Monitor; avoid nephrotoxins |
| 3b | 30–44 | Nephrology referral |
| 4 | 15–29 | Prepare for dialysis/transplant |
| 5 | < 15 | Dialysis/transplant |

## 4. Treatment

### 4.1 Slow progression

| Measure | Detail |
|---|---|
| BP control | Target < 130/80 if tolerated |
| ACEi/ARB | If proteinuria; monitor creatinine/K+ |
| Diabetes control | HbA1c < 7% (individualise) |
| Salt restriction | < 5 g/day |
| Protein | 0.8 g/kg/day (avoid high protein) |
| Smoking cessation | Essential |

### 4.2 Complications

| Problem | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Anaemia | Iron, erythropoietin (specialist) |
| Hyperphosphataemia | Phosphate binders, dietary restriction |
| Hypocalcaemia | Calcium + vitamin D |
| Metabolic acidosis | Sodium bicarbonate |
| Hyperkalaemia | Dietary restriction, binders |
| Fluid overload | Diuretics |

**Brand examples (illustrative):** Telmisartan — Telma; Amlodipine — Amlopres; Sevelamer — Renvela; Calcium + vitamin D — Shelcal.

## 5. Supportive care

- Medication review and dose adjustment
- Avoid NSAIDs, contrast, herbal nephrotoxins
- Vaccination (influenza, pneumococcal, hepatitis B)
- Dietitian counselling
- Psychosocial support
- Advance care planning in stage 5

## 6. Drug interactions and cautions

- **ACEi/ARB:** hyperkalaemia, rise in creatinine; avoid in pregnancy; hold in acute illness.
- **NSAIDs:** avoid — worsen CKD.
- **Metformin:** avoid if eGFR < 30.
- **Gabapentin/pregabalin:** dose adjust.
- **Contrast:** avoid unless essential; hydrate.
- **Potassium-sparing diuretics:** hyperkalaemia.
- **Herbal/ayurvedic products:** may contain heavy metals — ask.

## 7. Red flags — refer immediately

- eGFR < 30 or rapid decline
- Severe hyperkalaemia
- Pulmonary oedema
- Uremic symptoms (confusion, seizures, vomiting)
- Severe anaemia
- Suspected obstruction
- Pregnancy with CKD
- Uncontrolled BP despite 3 drugs

## 8. Follow-up

- Every 3–6 months (stage 3), 1–3 months (stage 4–5).
- Monitor creatinine, electrolytes, Hb, calcium/phosphate.
- Review medicines at every visit.
- Vaccination and lifestyle counselling.
