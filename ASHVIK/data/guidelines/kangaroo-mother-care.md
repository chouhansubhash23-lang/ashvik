# Kangaroo Mother Care (KMC)

> **Source:** WHO; RMNCH+A; NHP
> **Evidence:** `../../evidence/sources.md`
> **Last verified:** 2026-10-28
> **Status:** Draft — clinician review required
> **Setting:** Rural OPD / PHC (India)

## 1. Quick facts

- KMC = skin-to-skin contact + breastfeeding + support.
- Reduces mortality in low birth weight infants.
- Start as soon as stable.
- Continue at home.

## 2. Clinical features / eligibility

- Stable preterm/LBW
- Breathing spontaneously
- No severe illness
- Mother/caregiver available

## 3. Diagnosis / Assessment

| Assessment | Detail |
|---|---|
| Weight | LBW |
| Temperature | Stable |
| Breathing | Stable |
| Feeding | Breastfeeding/expressed milk |

## 4. Treatment: KMC practice

| Step | Detail |
|---|---|
| 1 | Baby in diaper, skin-to-skin on mother's chest |
| 2 | Cover with blanket |
| 3 | Continue 8–24 h/day |
| 4 | Breastfeed frequently |
| 5 | Monitor temperature |

**Paracetamol 500 mg** for maternal pain.

**Brand examples (illustrative):** Dolo 650.

## 5. Supportive care

- Family support
- Follow-up
- Hygiene

## 6. Drug interactions and cautions

- **Hypothermia:** monitor.
- **Apnoea:** stop KMC and resuscitate.
- **Infection:** treat.
- **Sedatives:** avoid in mother.

## 7. Red flags — refer immediately

- Apnoea
- Hypothermia
- Poor feeding
- Jaundice
- Sepsis signs

## 8. Follow-up

- Weight gain.
- Temperature.
- Immunisation.
