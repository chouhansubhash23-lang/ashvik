> **Last verified:** 2026-10-28

> **Setting:** Rural OPD / PHC (India)

## 1. Quick facts

- Most maternal deaths occur in the first 24 hours to 6 weeks.
- Check mother and baby at day 1, 3, 7, 14, 28.
- Screen for bleeding, infection, hypertension, and depression.
- Support breastfeeding and family planning.

## 2. Clinical features to assess

- Bleeding, lochia
- Fever, foul discharge
- Breast problems
- Perineal wound
- BP/headache
- Mood changes
- Newborn feeding/jaundice

## 3. Diagnosis / Assessment

| Test | Use |
|---|---|
| BP | Pre-eclampsia |
| Hb | Anaemia |
| Temperature | Infection |
| Breast exam | Mastitis |
| Newborn weight | Feeding |

## 4. Treatment

| Issue | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Anaemia | IFA 60 mg + 500 mcg folic acid |
| Pain | Paracetamol 500 mg |
| Infection | Antibiotics |
| Hypertension | Refer |
| Mastitis | Warm compress, antibiotics |

**Brand examples (illustrative):** Autrin; Dolo 650; Augmentin.

## 5. Supportive care

- Breastfeeding support
- Perineal hygiene
- Nutrition
- Contraception counselling
- Mental health screening

## 6. Drug interactions and cautions

- **NSAIDs:** avoid in bleeding.
- **Antibiotics:** renal adjustment.
- **OCPs:** caution in breastfeeding (progestin-only).
- **Iron:** constipation.

## 7. Red flags — refer immediately

- Heavy bleeding
- Foul lochia/fever
- Convulsions
- Severe headache
- Breast abscess
- Depression/suicidal ideation
- Newborn danger signs

## 8. Follow-up

- Postnatal visits.
- Newborn immunisation.
- Family planning.
