# Low Birth Weight (LBW) Care

> **Source:** WHO; RMNCH+A; NHP
> **Evidence:** `../../evidence/sources.md`
> **Last verified:** 2026-10-28
> **Status:** Draft — clinician review required
> **Setting:** Rural OPD / PHC (India)

## 1. Quick facts

- LBW < 2.5 kg; very LBW < 1.5 kg; extremely LBW < 1 kg.
- Keep warm, feed, prevent infection.
- KMC is central.
- Refer very LBW/preterm.

## 2. Clinical features

- Small size
- Poor feeding
- Hypothermia
- Jaundice
- Respiratory distress

## 3. Diagnosis / Assessment

| Assessment | Detail |
|---|---|
| Weight | Classification |
| Temperature | Hypothermia |
| Feeding | Suck/swallow |
| Glucose | Hypoglycaemia |
| Breathing | Distress |

## 4. Treatment

| Issue | Action |
|---|---|
| Hypothermia | KMC, warm |
| Feeding | Breastfeeding/expressed milk |
| Hypoglycaemia | 10% dextrose 2 mL/kg |
| Infection | Antibiotics |
| Referral | < 1.5 kg/preterm |

**Brand examples (illustrative):** 10% dextrose; Ampicillin; Gentamicin.

## 5. Supportive care

- KMC
- Hygiene
- Follow-up
- Family support

## 6. Drug interactions and cautions

- **Gentamicin:** dose by weight.
- **Dextrose:** monitor glucose.
- **Feeding:** avoid formula unless indicated.
- **Paracetamol:** not routine.

## 7. Red flags — refer immediately

- < 1.5 kg
- Respiratory distress
- Apnoea
- Hypoglycaemia
- Sepsis
- Poor feeding

## 8. Follow-up

- Weight gain.
- Growth monitoring.
- Immunisation.
