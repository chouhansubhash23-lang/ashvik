# Vitamin D Deficiency

> **Source:** ICMR; WHO; NHP
> **Evidence:** `../../evidence/sources.md`
> **Last verified:** 2026-10-28
> **Status:** Draft — clinician review required
> **Setting:** Rural OPD / PHC (India)

## 1. Quick facts

- Very common in India despite sunlight.
- Causes bone pain, myopathy, and fractures.
- Treat with cholecalciferol; check calcium.
- Maintenance doses prevent recurrence.

## 2. Clinical features

- Bone pain, muscle weakness
- Fatigue
- Rickets in children
- Osteomalacia in adults
- Recurrent fractures

## 3. Diagnosis / Assessment

| Test | Use |
|---|---|
| 25(OH)D | Deficiency (< 20 ng/mL) |
| Calcium, phosphate | Bone metabolism |
| PTH | Secondary hyperparathyroidism |
| X-ray | Rickets/osteomalacia |

## 4. Treatment

| Regimen | Dose |
|---|---|
| Deficiency | 60,000 IU weekly x 8 weeks |
| Maintenance | 800–2,000 IU daily |
| Severe deficiency | 60,000 IU weekly x 8–12 weeks |
| Calcium | 500 mg twice daily if deficient |

**Brand examples (illustrative):** Uprise D3; Calcirol; Shelcal.

## 5. Supportive care

- Sun exposure
- Diet (fortified foods)
- Calcium intake
- Weight-bearing exercise

## 6. Drug interactions and cautions

- **Vitamin D toxicity:** hypercalcaemia with excess.
- **Thiazides:** hypercalcaemia.
- **Calcium:** constipation.
- **Anticonvulsants:** increase requirement.

## 7. Red flags — refer immediately

- Severe bone pain/fracture
- Hypercalcaemia
- Renal stones
- Suspected malignancy

## 8. Follow-up

- Recheck 25(OH)D after 3 months.
- Maintain normal levels.
- Bone health review.
