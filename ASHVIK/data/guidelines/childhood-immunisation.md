# Childhood Immunisation (National Immunisation Schedule)

> **Source:** Universal Immunisation Programme (UIP), MoHFW; WHO
> **Evidence:** `../../evidence/sources.md`
> **Last verified:** 2026-10-27
> **Status:** Draft — clinician review required
> **Setting:** Rural OPD / PHC (India)

## 1. Quick facts

- UIP protects against 12 vaccine-preventable diseases.
- Vaccines are free at government facilities.
- Track every child with a card; catch up missed doses.
- Minor fever/URI is **not** a contraindication.

## 2. Diagnosis / Assessment and schedule (UIP)

| Age | Vaccines |
|---|---|
| At birth | BCG, OPV-0, Hepatitis B birth dose |
| 6 weeks | Pentavalent-1 (DPT+HepB+Hib), OPV-1, Rotavirus-1, PCV-1, fIPV-1 |
| 10 weeks | Pentavalent-2, OPV-2, Rotavirus-2, PCV-2 |
| 14 weeks | Pentavalent-3, OPV-3, Rotavirus-3, PCV-3, fIPV-2 |
| 9–12 months | MR-1, JE-1 (endemic districts), PCV booster |
| 16–24 months | MR-2, JE-2, DPT booster-1, OPV booster |
| 5–6 years | DPT booster-2 |
| 10 years | Td |
| 16 years | Td |
| Pregnant women | TT/Td as per schedule |

*State schedules may vary; follow the local UIP chart.*

## 3. Catch-up and missed doses

- Resume from where stopped; do not restart the schedule.
- Minimum intervals: DPT/Pentavalent 4 weeks; OPV 4 weeks; MR 4 weeks.
- Unvaccinated child > 1 year: start from the beginning with age-appropriate vaccines.

## 4. Treatment: Adverse events following immunisation (AEFI)

| Reaction | Management |
|---|---|
| Local pain/swelling | Cold compress, paracetamol |
| Fever | Paracetamol 15 mg/kg/dose |
| Abscess | Drain, antibiotics |
| Anaphylaxis | Adrenaline 0.01 mg/kg IM (max 0.5 mg), oxygen, refer |
| Seizure/faint | Position, observe, refer |

**Report all serious AEFI** to the district immunisation officer.

## 5. Contraindications

- Severe allergic reaction to a previous dose or component
- Live vaccines in severe immunocompromise (specialist advice)
- Acute severe illness with fever (defer until stable)

**Not contraindications:** mild fever, URI, diarrhoea, malnutrition, breastfeeding.

## 6. Drug interactions and cautions

- **Live vaccines** (BCG, OPV, MR, JE live): avoid in pregnancy/immunocompromise; space with immunoglobulin.
- **Steroids/chemotherapy:** live vaccines contraindicated during high-dose immunosuppression.
- **Paracetamol:** safe for post-vaccine fever; max 4 g/day adult.
- **Adrenaline:** keep ready for anaphylaxis.

## 7. Red flags — refer immediately

- Anaphylaxis
- Persistent seizure
- Encephalopathy
- Severe local reaction with necrosis
- High fever > 3 days
- Sepsis-like illness

## 8. Follow-up

- Give next appointment date.
- Record in immunisation card and register.
- Track defaulters with ASHA/ANM.
- Counsel on nutrition and growth monitoring.
