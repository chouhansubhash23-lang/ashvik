# Eye and ENT Infections

> **Source:** ICMR STW Vol. 3 (2022); NHP; WHO
> **Evidence:** `../../evidence/sources.md`
> **Last verified:** 2026-10-27
> **Status:** Draft — clinician review required
> **Setting:** Rural OPD / PHC (India)

## 1. Quick facts

- Common: conjunctivitis, stye, otitis media/externa, sinusitis, tonsillitis, epistaxis.
- **Never** use steroid eye drops without a slit-lamp diagnosis.
- Red-flag eye and ear conditions need same-day referral.
- Avoid unnecessary antibiotics; most conjunctivitis and colds are viral.

## 2. Clinical features

| Condition | Features |
|---|---|
| Bacterial conjunctivitis | Purulent discharge, sticky eyelids |
| Viral conjunctivitis | Watery discharge, redness, itching |
| Allergic conjunctivitis | Itching, watery eyes, seasonal |
| Stye (hordeolum) | Painful lid swelling |
| Blepharitis | Crusting, lid margin redness |
| Corneal ulcer | Severe pain, photophobia, vision loss, white spot |
| Otitis media | Ear pain, fever, bulging tympanic membrane (children) |
| Otitis externa | Ear pain, discharge, tenderness on pulling pinna |
| Sinusitis | Facial pain, nasal discharge, headache |
| Tonsillitis | Sore throat, fever, enlarged tonsils |
| Epistaxis | Nose bleeding |

## 3. Diagnosis

| Test | Use |
|---|---|
| Clinical exam | Most cases |
| Otoscopy | Otitis media/externa |
| Visual acuity | All eye complaints |
| Fluorescein stain | Corneal ulcer (if available) |
| Throat swab | Recurrent tonsillitis |

## 4. Treatment

### 4.1 Eye

| Condition | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Bacterial conjunctivitis | Chloramphenicol 0.5% drops 4 times daily x 5–7 days (or ofloxacin) |
| Viral conjunctivitis | Lubricants; hygiene; self-limiting |
| Allergic conjunctivitis | Antihistamine drops (olopatadine), cold compress |
| Stye | Warm compress; topical antibiotic; incision if large |
| Blepharitis | Lid hygiene; warm compresses |
| Dry eye | Artificial tears |

**Brand examples (illustrative):** Chloramphenicol — Chloromycetin; Ofloxacin — Oflox; Olopatadine — Patanol.

**Do not use steroid drops** unless diagnosed by an eye specialist.

### 4.2 Ear

| Condition | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Otitis media | Amoxicillin 40–45 mg/kg/day (children) or amoxicillin-clavulanate; analgesia |
| Otitis externa | Ciprofloxacin ear drops; keep ear dry; avoid ear buds |
| Wax impaction | Safe syringing if trained; otherwise refer |

**Brand examples:** Amoxicillin — Mox; Ciprofloxacin ear drops — Ciplox.

### 4.3 Nose/throat

| Condition | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Sinusitis | Saline irrigation, decongestant (short-term), amoxicillin-clavulanate if bacterial |
| Tonsillitis | Analgesia, fluids; penicillin/amoxicillin if bacterial |
| Epistaxis | Pinch nose, lean forward, ice; cautery/packing if persistent |
| Allergic rhinitis | Antihistamine, nasal steroid |

**Brand examples:** Amoxicillin-clavulanate — Augmentin; Xylometazoline — Otrivin; Cetirizine — Cetzine.

## 5. Supportive care

- Hand hygiene; avoid sharing towels.
- Warm compresses for stye.
- Avoid ear buds and self-medication.
- Treat allergies.
- Vaccination (pneumococcal, influenza) for recurrent otitis media.

## 6. Drug interactions and cautions

- **Chloramphenicol eye drops:** rare aplastic anaemia with prolonged use; avoid in pregnancy? (topical low risk).
- **Ofloxacin eye drops:** local irritation; avoid contact lenses during treatment.
- **Amoxicillin:** rash in infectious mononucleosis.
- **Amoxicillin-clavulanate:** diarrhoea, cholestatic jaundice.
- **Decongestants:** avoid in hypertension, glaucoma, MAOI use; limit to 3–5 days.
- **Aminoglycoside ear drops:** avoid if tympanic membrane perforation.

## 7. Red flags — refer immediately

- Sudden vision loss or severe eye pain
- Corneal ulcer / white spot
- Eye trauma / chemical injury
- Proptosis, ophthalmoplegia
- Mastoiditis (swelling behind ear)
- Facial palsy with ear infection
- Epiglottitis (drooling, stridor, unable to swallow)
- Deep neck space infection
- Persistent unilateral nasal obstruction/bleeding

## 8. Follow-up

- Review in 48–72 h for eye/ear infections.
- Complete antibiotic course.
- Refer if no improvement.
- Hearing assessment after recurrent otitis media.
