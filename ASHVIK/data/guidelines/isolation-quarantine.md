# Isolation and Quarantine

> **Source:** NCDC; WHO; NHP
> **Evidence:** `../../evidence/sources.md`
> **Last verified:** 2026-10-28
> **Status:** Draft — clinician review required
> **Setting:** Rural OPD / PHC (India)

## 1. Quick facts

- Isolation separates sick people; quarantine separates exposed people.
- Duration depends on disease.
- Ensure dignity, consent, and support.
- Follow legal provisions.

## 2. Indications

| Disease | Measure |
|---|---|
| TB | Respiratory isolation until non-infectious |
| Measles | Isolation |
| COVID-19 | Isolation |
| Cholera | Enteric precautions |
| Meningococcal | Droplet precautions |

## 3. Diagnosis / Assessment

| Assessment | Detail |
|---|---|
| Infectious period | Duration |
| Symptoms | Severity |
| Contacts | Quarantine |
| Home feasibility | Safety |

## 4. Treatment / support

| Measure | Detail |
|---|---|
| PPE | Staff |
| Ventilation | Rooms |
| Hygiene | Handwashing |
| Paracetamol 500 mg | Symptomatic |

**Brand examples (illustrative):** Dolo 650; ORS.

## 5. Supportive care

- Food/water
- Mental health
- Communication
- Stigma reduction

## 6. Drug interactions and cautions

- **PPE:** correct use.
- **Sedatives:** avoid.
- **Isolation:** monitor for deterioration.
- **Legal:** follow public health law.

## 7. Red flags — escalate

- Deterioration
- Non-compliance
- Large outbreak
- Vulnerable person

## 8. Follow-up

- End isolation per criteria.
- Document.
- Support.
