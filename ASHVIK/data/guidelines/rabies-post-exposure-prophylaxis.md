# Rabies Post-Exposure Prophylaxis (PEP)

> **Source:** National Rabies Control Programme; WHO; NHP
> **Evidence:** `../../evidence/sources.md`
> **Last verified:** 2026-10-28
> **Status:** Draft — clinician review required
> **Setting:** Rural OPD / PHC (India)

## 1. Quick facts

- Rabies is nearly 100% fatal once symptoms start — PEP is life-saving.
- **Wash the wound immediately with soap and water for 15 minutes.**
- Give vaccine and (for category III) rabies immunoglobulin.
- Do not delay PEP; do not suture immediately.

## 2. Wound categories

| Category | Exposure | PEP |
|---|---|---|
| I | Touching/feeding, intact skin | No PEP |
| II | Nibbling, superficial scratch | Vaccine |
| III | Bite, scratch with bleeding, mucosal/saliva contact | Vaccine + RIG |

## 3. Diagnosis / Assessment

- Animal type (dog, cat, bat, wild)
- Wound category
- Vaccination status of animal (observe 10 days if possible)
- Tetanus status
- Allergy history

## 4. Treatment

| Step | Detail |
|---|---|
| 1 | Wash 15 min with soap and water |
| 2 | Apply antiseptic (povidone-iodine) |
| 3 | Do not suture immediately |
| 4 | Vaccine (IM) days 0, 3, 7, 14, 28 |
| 5 | RIG for category III (infiltrate wound) |
| 6 | Tetanus prophylaxis |

**Vaccine dose:** 0.5 mL or 1 mL IM as per product (deltoid).
**RIG:** infiltrate around wound; dose as per product.

**Brand examples (illustrative):** Rabies vaccine (Verorab, Rabipur); RIG (Equirab, Berirab).

## 5. Supportive care

- Analgesia (paracetamol 500 mg)
- Wound review
- Observe animal if possible
- Counselling

## 6. Drug interactions and cautions

- **RIG:** do not inject into wound if healed; infiltrate.
- **Vaccine:** local pain, fever; complete schedule.
- **Immunosuppression:** may need additional doses.
- **Pregnancy:** PEP is safe and indicated.

## 7. Red flags — refer immediately

- Category III bite
- Bite to face/hands/genitals
- Bat exposure
- Wild animal bite
- Symptoms of rabies (hydrophobia, aerophobia)
- Immunocompromised

## 8. Follow-up

- Complete vaccine schedule.
- Wound review.
- Tetanus booster.
