# Gestational Diabetes Mellitus (GDM)

> **Source:** ICMR; WHO; NHP
> **Evidence:** `../../evidence/sources.md`
> **Last verified:** 2026-10-28
> **Status:** Draft — clinician review required
> **Setting:** Rural OPD / PHC (India)

## 1. Quick facts

- GDM = glucose intolerance first detected in pregnancy.
- Screen at 24–28 weeks (earlier if high risk).
- Lifestyle is first-line; insulin if uncontrolled.
- Postpartum follow-up for diabetes.

## 2. Clinical features

- Often asymptomatic
- Polyuria/polydipsia
- Large baby (polyhydramnios)
- Recurrent infections

## 3. Diagnosis / Assessment

| Test | Use |
|---|---|
| OGTT 75 g | Diagnosis |
| Fasting/postprandial glucose | Monitoring |
| HbA1c | Baseline |
| Ultrasound | Fetal growth |

## 4. Treatment

| Step | Detail |
|---|---|
| Diet | Complex carbs, portion control |
| Exercise | 30 min/day |
| Glucose targets | Fasting < 95, 2-h < 120 mg/dL |
| Insulin | If targets not met |

**Insulin:** basal-bolus or NPH (specialist).
**Metformin:** selected cases (specialist).

**Brand examples (illustrative):** Human Actrapid; Mixtard; Glycomet.

## 5. Supportive care

- Nutrition counselling
- Glucose monitoring
- Fetal surveillance
- Postpartum OGTT

## 6. Drug interactions and cautions

- **Insulin:** hypoglycaemia.
- **Metformin:** GI upset; specialist.
- **Steroids:** raise glucose.
- **Paracetamol 500 mg** for pain.

## 7. Red flags — refer immediately

- Severe hyperglycaemia
- Ketoacidosis
- Fetal distress
- Pre-eclampsia
- Polyhydramnios

## 8. Follow-up

- Postpartum OGTT at 6–12 weeks.
- Annual screening.
- Lifestyle counselling.
