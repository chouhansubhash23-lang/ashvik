# Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD) Screening

> **Source:** RBSK; WHO; NHP
> **Evidence:** `../../evidence/sources.md`
> **Last verified:** 2026-10-28
> **Status:** Draft — clinician review required
> **Setting:** Rural OPD / PHC (India)

## 1. Quick facts

- Early recognition enables early intervention.
- Red flags: no eye contact, no response to name, no pointing.
- Screen at 18 and 24 months.
- Refer for developmental assessment.

## 2. Clinical features

- Poor eye contact
- No response to name by 12 months
- No pointing/showing by 18 months
- No pretend play
- Repetitive behaviours
- Language delay
- Sensory sensitivities

## 3. Diagnosis / Assessment

| Tool | Use |
|---|---|
| M-CHAT-R | Screening (16–30 months) |
| Developmental milestones | All visits |
| Hearing test | Exclude hearing loss |
| Vision test | Exclude visual impairment |

## 4. Treatment

| Finding | Action |
|---|---|
| Positive screen | Refer for assessment |
| Language delay | Speech therapy |
| Behaviour concerns | Behavioural intervention |
| Seizures | AED |

**Brand examples (illustrative):** No specific drug; treat comorbidities (e.g., melatonin 3 mg for sleep).

## 5. Supportive care

- Parent training
- Structured routine
- Speech/occupational therapy
- School support

## 6. Drug interactions and cautions

- **Melatonin:** sedation; use short-term.
- **Antipsychotics:** metabolic effects; specialist.
- **Stimulants:** specialist.
- **Avoid unproven therapies.**

## 7. Red flags — refer immediately

- Regression of skills
- Seizures
- Self-injury
- Severe aggression
- Suspected hearing loss

## 8. Follow-up

- Regular developmental review.
- Early intervention.
- Family support.
