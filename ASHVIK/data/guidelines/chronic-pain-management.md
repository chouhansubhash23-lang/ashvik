# Chronic Pain Management

> **Source:** ICMR; WHO; NHP
> **Evidence:** `../../evidence/sources.md`
> **Last verified:** 2026-10-28
> **Status:** Draft — clinician review required
> **Setting:** Rural OPD / PHC (India)

## 1. Quick facts

- Chronic pain lasts > 3 months; biopsychosocial approach.
- Start with non-drug measures and non-opioid analgesics.
- Avoid long-term NSAIDs in elderly/renal disease.
- Opioids only when indicated, with monitoring.

## 2. Clinical features

- Persistent pain, impact on function/sleep/mood
- Neuropathic features (burning, electric shocks)
- Musculoskeletal pain
- Headache
- Depression/anxiety

## 3. Diagnosis / Assessment

| Assessment | Detail |
|---|---|
| Pain score | 0–10 |
| Function | ADL, sleep |
| Mood | PHQ-9 |
| Red flags | Cancer/infection/fracture |
| Medication review | Analgesic overuse |

## 4. Treatment

| Step | Treatment |
|---|---|
| 1 | Paracetamol 500–650 mg TDS |
| 2 | Topical NSAID |
| 3 | Oral NSAID short-term (caution) |
| 4 | Neuropathic: amitriptyline 10–25 mg HS or gabapentin 300 mg HS |
| 5 | Opioid (selected cases) |

**Brand examples (illustrative):** Dolo 650; Voveran gel; Tryptomer; Gabapin.

## 5. Supportive care

- Exercise/physiotherapy
- Sleep hygiene
- CBT/relaxation
- Weight management

## 6. Drug interactions and cautions

- **NSAIDs:** GI bleed, kidney injury, hypertension.
- **Amitriptyline:** anticholinergic, arrhythmia.
- **Gabapentin:** sedation, renal dose.
- **Opioids:** dependence, constipation.

## 7. Red flags — refer immediately

- New severe pain
- Neurological deficit
- Unexplained weight loss
- Fever with pain
- Cancer history

## 8. Follow-up

- Reassess pain/function.
- Review medications.
- Multidisciplinary referral.
