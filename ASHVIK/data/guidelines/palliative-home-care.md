# Palliative Home Care

> **Source:** National Palliative Care Programme; ICMR; WHO
> **Evidence:** `../../evidence/sources.md`
> **Last verified:** 2026-10-28
> **Status:** Draft — clinician review required
> **Setting:** Rural OPD / PHC (India)

## 1. Quick facts

- Most patients prefer to be cared for at home.
- Train the caregiver and provide a written plan.
- Ensure access to pain relief and emergency contacts.
- Coordinate with ASHA/ANM and local services.

## 2. Clinical features

- Pain, breathlessness
- Poor appetite, weight loss
- Fatigue, weakness
- Pressure ulcers
- Anxiety/depression

## 3. Diagnosis / Assessment

| Domain | Assess |
|---|---|
| Pain | Severity, triggers |
| Breathlessness | SpO2, effort |
| Function | Mobility, self-care |
| Skin | Pressure areas |
| Caregiver | Capacity, support |
| Home | Safety, water, electricity |

## 4. Treatment

| Symptom | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Pain | Paracetamol 500 mg; morphine 5–10 mg q4h if severe |
| Breathlessness | Oxygen if hypoxic; low-dose morphine |
| Nausea | Ondansetron 4 mg |
| Constipation | Senna/lactulose |
| Pressure ulcer | Repositioning, dressing |

**Brand examples (illustrative):** Morphine; Ondansetron — Emeset; Duphalac.

## 5. Supportive care

- Caregiver training
- Nutrition/hydration
- Emotional support
- Bereavement support

## 6. Drug interactions and cautions

- **Morphine:** constipation, sedation; laxative.
- **Ondansetron:** QT prolongation.
- **Sedatives:** respiratory depression.
- **Opioid storage:** secure.

## 7. Red flags — refer immediately

- Uncontrolled pain
- Severe breathlessness
- Bleeding
- Spinal cord compression
- Caregiver crisis
- Patient safety risk

## 8. Follow-up

- Regular home visits/calls.
- Reassess symptoms.
- Support caregiver.
