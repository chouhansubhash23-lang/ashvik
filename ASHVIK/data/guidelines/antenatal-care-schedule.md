# Antenatal Care (ANC) Schedule

> **Source:** RMNCH+A / NHM; WHO; NHP
> **Evidence:** `../../evidence/sources.md`
> **Last verified:** 2026-10-28
> **Status:** Draft — clinician review required
> **Setting:** Rural OPD / PHC (India)

## 1. Quick facts

- Early registration and at least 4 ANC visits improve outcomes.
- Screen for anaemia, hypertension, and infections.
- Provide IFA, calcium, and TT/Td.
- Counsel on danger signs and birth preparedness.

## 2. Visit schedule

| Visit | Timing | Key actions |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | < 12 weeks | Register, history, BP, weight, Hb, urine, blood group, HIV/HBsAg/VDRL, TT/Td, IFA, calcium, folic acid |
| 2 | 14–20 weeks | BP, weight, Hb, fetal heart, anomaly scan |
| 3 | 28–32 weeks | BP, Hb, urine, growth scan |
| 4 | 36–40 weeks | BP, presentation, birth plan |

## 3. Diagnosis / Assessment

| Test | Use |
|---|---|
| Hb | Anaemia |
| BP/urine | Pre-eclampsia |
| Blood group | Rh incompatibility |
| HIV/HBsAg/VDRL | Infection |
| Ultrasound | Dating/anomaly |

## 4. Treatment

| Supplement | Dose |
|---|---|
| IFA | 1 tablet daily (60 mg iron + 500 mcg folic acid) x 180 days |
| Calcium | 500 mg twice daily from 14 weeks |
| Folic acid | 5 mg daily preconception/first trimester |
| TT/Td | As per schedule |

**Brand examples (illustrative):** Autrin; Shelcal; Folvite.

## 5. Supportive care

- Nutrition counselling
- Danger-sign education
- Birth preparedness
- ASHA/ANM linkage

## 6. Drug interactions and cautions

- **Iron:** separate from calcium/antacids.
- **Calcium:** constipation.
- **ACEi/ARB:** contraindicated.
- **Paracetamol 500 mg** for pain/fever.

## 7. Red flags — refer immediately

- Bleeding
- Severe headache/blurred vision
- Convulsions
- Reduced fetal movements
- Leaking fluid
- Fever
- Severe abdominal pain

## 8. Follow-up

- Visit schedule.
- Hb/BP monitoring.
- Postnatal planning.
