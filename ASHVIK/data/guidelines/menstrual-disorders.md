# Menstrual Disorders (Dysmenorrhoea, AUB, PCOS)

> **Source:** ICMR STW Vol. 3 (2022); NHP; WHO
> **Evidence:** `../../evidence/sources.md`
> **Last verified:** 2026-10-27
> **Status:** Draft — clinician review required
> **Setting:** Rural OPD / PHC (India)

## 1. Quick facts

- Common: dysmenorrhoea, heavy menstrual bleeding (AUB), irregular cycles, PCOS.
- Always exclude pregnancy, thyroid disease, and anaemia.
- AUB in women > 45 needs endometrial evaluation.
- PCOS needs lifestyle management and long-term follow-up.

## 2. Clinical features

| Condition | Features |
|---|---|
| Primary dysmenorrhoea | Painful periods, no pelvic pathology, young women |
| Secondary dysmenorrhoea | Pain with endometriosis, fibroids, PID |
| AUB | Heavy/prolonged/irregular bleeding |
| PCOS | Oligo/anovulation, hirsutism, acne, obesity |
| Menopause | Irregular cycles, hot flushes, mood changes |

## 3. Diagnosis

| Test | Use |
|---|---|
| Pregnancy test | All women of reproductive age |
| CBC | Anaemia |
| TSH | Thyroid dysfunction |
| Pelvic ultrasound | Fibroids, PCOS, endometrium |
| Hormonal profile | FSH/LH, testosterone (PCOS) |
| Endometrial biopsy | AUB > 45 or risk factors |

## 4. Treatment

### 4.1 Dysmenorrhoea

| Drug | Brand example (illustrative) | Dose |
|---|---|---|
| Ibuprofen | Brufen | 400 mg three times daily with food |
| Mefenamic acid | Meftal | 500 mg three times daily |
| Combined OCP | Mala-N | Cyclical, if contraception needed |

Start NSAID 1–2 days before menses.

### 4.2 Heavy menstrual bleeding (AUB)

| Measure | Detail |
|---|---|
| Iron | Treat anaemia |
| Tranexamic acid | 500 mg three times daily during menses (max 5 days) |
| NSAID | Mefenamic acid |
| Combined OCP | Cyclical |
| Levonorgestrel IUCD | Specialist |
| Referral | Fibroids, hyperplasia, no response |

**Brand examples:** Tranexamic acid — Texid; Mefenamic acid — Meftal.

### 4.3 PCOS

| Measure | Detail |
|---|---|
| Lifestyle | Weight loss 5–10%, exercise |
| Metformin | 500 mg BD (insulin resistance) |
| Combined OCP | Cycle regulation, hirsutism |
| Referral | Fertility, severe hirsutism |

### 4.4 Menopause

- Lifestyle, calcium/vitamin D, exercise.
- HRT only after specialist assessment.
- Screen for osteoporosis and cardiovascular risk.

## 5. Supportive care

- Menstrual hygiene education
- Iron-rich diet
- Pain relief and heat
- Contraception counselling
- Mental health support

## 6. Drug interactions and cautions

- **NSAIDs:** GI bleed, kidney injury; avoid in pregnancy.
- **Tranexamic acid:** avoid in active thromboembolic disease; caution in renal impairment.
- **OCPs:** see contraception chapter; contraindicated in smokers > 35, migraine with aura, VTE.
- **Metformin:** GI upset; hold before contrast.
- **Iron:** constipation; separate from calcium/antacids.

## 7. Red flags — refer immediately

- Heavy bleeding with hemodynamic instability
- Postmenopausal bleeding
- Intermenstrual bleeding
- Severe pelvic pain with fever (PID)
- Suspected ectopic pregnancy
- AUB with fibroids/hyperplasia
- PCOS with infertility
- Virilisation

## 8. Follow-up

- Review after 3 months.
- Recheck Hb after iron therapy.
- Ultrasound if not improving.
- Fertility counselling when desired.
