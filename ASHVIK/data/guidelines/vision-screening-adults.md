# Vision Screening in Adults

> **Source:** WHO; NPCB; NHP
> **Evidence:** `../../evidence/sources.md`
> **Last verified:** 2026-10-28
> **Status:** Draft — clinician review required
> **Setting:** Rural OPD / PHC (India)

## 1. Quick facts

- Screen for refractive error, cataract, glaucoma, and retinopathy.
- Vision loss affects safety and quality of life.
- Refer for surgery/treatment.
- Screen diabetics annually.

## 2. Clinical features

- Blurred vision
- Difficulty reading
- Eye pain/redness
- Floaters/flashes
- Visual field loss

## 3. Diagnosis / Assessment

| Test | Use |
|---|---|
| Visual acuity (Snellen) | Baseline |
| Pinhole | Refractive error |
| Fundus | Retina |
| Eye pressure | Glaucoma (referral) |
| Slit lamp | Anterior segment |

## 4. Treatment

| Cause | Action |
|---|---|
| Refractive error | Glasses |
| Cataract | Surgery referral |
| Glaucoma | Refer |
| Diabetic retinopathy | Refer |
| Dry eye | Lubricants |

- **Paracetamol 500 mg** for eye pain (after excluding serious cause).

**Brand examples (illustrative):** Artificial tears; Dolo 650.

## 5. Supportive care

- Eye protection
- Diabetes control
- Nutrition (vitamin A)
- Regular screening

## 6. Drug interactions and cautions

- **Steroid eye drops:** avoid without diagnosis.
- **Anticholinergics:** acute glaucoma risk.
- **Anticoagulants:** bleeding.
- **Contact lenses:** hygiene.

## 7. Red flags — refer immediately

- Sudden vision loss
- Severe eye pain
- Red eye with vision loss
- Chemical injury
- Retinal detachment symptoms

## 8. Follow-up

- Annual screening.
- Glasses review.
- Diabetic eye exam.
