# Blood Culture Collection

> **Source:** ICMR; WHO; NHP
> **Evidence:** `../../evidence/sources.md`
> **Last verified:** 2026-10-28
> **Status:** Draft — clinician review required
> **Setting:** Rural OPD / PHC (India)

## 1. Quick facts

- Blood cultures guide antibiotic therapy.
- Collect before antibiotics.
- Use aseptic technique; adequate volume.
- Two sets from different sites.

## 2. Indications

- Suspected sepsis
- Fever with rigors
- Immunocompromised
- Endocarditis suspicion

## 3. Diagnosis / Assessment

- Timing: before antibiotics, during chills
- Volume: 10–20 mL per set (adult)
- Sites: two peripheral veins
- Skin antisepsis: chlorhexidine/alcohol

## 4. Treatment: collection steps

| Step | Detail |
|---|---|
| 1 | Hand hygiene, gloves |
| 2 | Antisepsis, allow to dry |
| 3 | Draw 10–20 mL per bottle set |
| 4 | Inoculate aerobic/anaerobic bottles |
| 5 | Label and send promptly |
| 6 | Do not delay antibiotics in sepsis |

**Paracetamol 500 mg** for fever after cultures.

**Brand examples (illustrative):** Dolo 650.

## 5. Supportive care

- Monitor vitals
- Repeat cultures if persistent fever
- Document

## 6. Drug interactions and cautions

- **Antibiotics:** reduce yield if collected after.
- **Antiseptic contamination:** false positives.
- **Volume:** low volume reduces sensitivity.
- **Delay:** do not delay sepsis treatment.

## 7. Red flags — refer immediately

- Sepsis/shock
- Suspected endocarditis
- Immunocompromised with fever

## 8. Follow-up

- Culture results.
- De-escalate antibiotics.
- Repeat if needed.
