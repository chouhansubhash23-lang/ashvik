# Adult Obesity

> **Source:** ICMR; WHO; NHP
> **Evidence:** `../../evidence/sources.md`
> **Last verified:** 2026-10-28
> **Status:** Draft — clinician review required
> **Setting:** Rural OPD / PHC (India)

## 1. Quick facts

- BMI ≥ 25 kg/m² (Asian cut-off) = overweight; ≥ 30 = obesity.
- Lifestyle change is first-line.
- Screen for diabetes, hypertension, dyslipidaemia, sleep apnoea.
- Refer for bariatric surgery in selected cases.

## 2. Clinical features

- Weight gain, central adiposity
- Snoring/daytime sleepiness
- Joint pain
- Breathlessness
- Skin changes (acanthosis)

## 3. Diagnosis / Assessment

| Measure | Use |
|---|---|
| BMI | Classification |
| Waist circumference | Central obesity |
| BP | Hypertension |
| Fasting glucose/HbA1c | Diabetes |
| Lipids | Dyslipidaemia |
| Sleep study | Apnoea (referral) |

## 4. Treatment

| Measure | Detail |
|---|---|
| Diet | 500–750 kcal/day deficit |
| Exercise | 150–300 min/week |
| Behaviour | Self-monitoring, support |
| Pharmacotherapy | Orlistat 120 mg TDS (selected) |
| Surgery | BMI ≥ 40 or ≥ 35 with comorbidity |

**Brand examples (illustrative):** Orlistat — Xenical; Vitamin D 800 IU.

## 5. Supportive care

- Dietitian
- Physical activity
- Sleep hygiene
- Mental health

## 6. Drug interactions and cautions

- **Orlistat:** steatorrhoea; fat-soluble vitamin deficiency.
- **Metformin:** GI upset.
- **Weight-loss supplements:** unsafe.
- **SGLT2 inhibitors:** genital infections.

## 7. Red flags — refer immediately

- Severe obesity with apnoea
- Chest pain
- Suspected Cushing/hypothyroidism
- Bariatric complications

## 8. Follow-up

- Monthly weight.
- Comorbidity screening.
- Lifestyle reinforcement.
