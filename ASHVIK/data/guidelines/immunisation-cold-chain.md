# Immunisation Cold Chain

> **Source:** Universal Immunisation Programme (UIP); WHO; NHP
> **Evidence:** `../../evidence/sources.md`
> **Last verified:** 2026-10-28
> **Status:** Draft — clinician review required
> **Setting:** Rural OPD / PHC (India)

## 1. Quick facts

- Vaccines must be kept at **2–8 °C** (most) or frozen (OPV) as per protocol.
- Heat and freezing both damage vaccines.
- Monitor temperature twice daily and record.
- Use vaccine vial monitors (VVM) and check expiry.

## 2. Diagnosis / Assessment

- Check cold chain equipment (ILR, deep freezer, vaccine carrier).
- Review temperature logs.
- Inspect VVM and expiry.
- Check shake test for freeze-sensitive vaccines (Hep B, DPT, TT, PCV).

## 3. Treatment: cold chain practice

| Item | Requirement |
|---|---|
| ILR | 2–8 °C; water bottles for stabilization |
| Deep freezer | −15 to −25 °C (OPV) |
| Vaccine carrier | Ice packs, conditioned |
| Temperature log | Twice daily |
| VVM | Check before use |

- Do not store food/drugs in ILR.
- Keep vaccines away from freezer compartment.
- Use open vial policy only for specified vaccines.

## 4. Drug interactions and cautions

- **Freezing:** damages Hep B, DPT, TT, PCV — discard if frozen.
- **Heat:** damages OPV, measles — use VVM.
- **Dose:** not applicable (non-drug topic).
- **Paracetamol 500 mg** for post-vaccine fever.

## 5. Supportive care

- Train staff on cold chain.
- Backup power/ice packs.
- Emergency plan for power failure.

## 6. Red flags

- Temperature out of range
- VVM reached discard point
- Frozen freeze-sensitive vaccine
- Expired vaccine
- Broken cold chain during outreach

## 7. Follow-up

- Record and audit logs.
- Report cold chain breaches.
- Replace stock as per protocol.
