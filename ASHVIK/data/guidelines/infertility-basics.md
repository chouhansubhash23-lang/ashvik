# Infertility — Basic Assessment

> **Source:** ICMR STW Vol. 3 (2022); WHO; NHP
> **Evidence:** `../../evidence/sources.md`
> **Last verified:** 2026-10-27
> **Status:** Draft — clinician review required
> **Setting:** Rural OPD / PHC (India)

## 1. Quick facts

- Infertility = failure to conceive after 12 months of regular unprotected intercourse.
- Assess **both partners**.
- Common causes: ovulation disorders (PCOS), tubal factor, male factor, unexplained.
- Refer for assisted reproduction when indicated.

## 2. Clinical assessment

| Partner | History |
|---|---|
| Female | Age, cycle regularity, dysmenorrhoea, PID/STI, surgeries, TB, thyroid |
| Male | Libido, erection/ejaculation, varicocele, mumps, alcohol/smoking, occupation/heat |
| Both | Duration, frequency, previous pregnancies, family history |

## 3. Diagnosis

| Test | Partner | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Semen analysis | Male | First-line; repeat after 3 months if abnormal |
| Ovulation tracking | Female | Cycle history, mid-luteal progesterone |
| TSH, prolactin | Female | Endocrine causes |
| Pelvic ultrasound | Female | PCOS, fibroids, ovarian reserve |
| HSG / tubal patency | Female | Tubal blockage |
| Rubella/HIV/HBV | Both | Preconception screening |

## 4. Treatment

### 4.1 Lifestyle

- Weight optimisation, stop smoking/alcohol
- Folic acid 5 mg daily (preconception)
- Timed intercourse (every 2–3 days around ovulation)
- Reduce heat exposure for male partner

### 4.2 Ovulation induction

| Drug | Brand example (illustrative) | Dose | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Letrozole | Letroz | 2.5–5 mg daily x 5 days | First-line in PCOS |
| Clomiphene citrate | Clomid | 50–100 mg daily x 5 days | Specialist monitoring |
| Metformin | Glycomet | 500 mg BD | Insulin resistance/PCOS |

### 4.3 Specific causes

| Cause | Management |
|---|---|
| PCOS | Weight loss, letrozole/metformin, refer |
| Tubal block | Refer for IVF/ICSI |
| Male factor | Refer andrology/IVF |
| Endometriosis | Refer |
| Thyroid/prolactin | Treat and recheck |

## 5. Supportive care

- Couple counselling; reduce anxiety
- Confidentiality
- Financial and emotional support
- Avoid unproven remedies

## 6. Drug interactions and cautions

- **Letrozole/clomiphene:** multiple pregnancy, ovarian hyperstimulation; specialist monitoring.
- **Metformin:** GI upset; hold before contrast.
- **Folic acid:** 5 mg for preconception; safe.
- **Avoid:** anabolic steroids, unnecessary hormones.

## 7. Red flags — refer immediately

- Age > 35 with 6 months of trying
- Irregular/absent periods
- Severe dysmenorrhoea/endometriosis
- History of PID/STI
- Abnormal semen analysis
- Recurrent pregnancy loss
- Galactorrhoea/thyroid symptoms
- Male varicocele/testicular atrophy

## 8. Follow-up

- Review after 3 months of lifestyle/treatment.
- Repeat semen analysis.
- Refer if no conception after 6–12 months of appropriate management.
