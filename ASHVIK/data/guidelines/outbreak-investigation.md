# Outbreak Investigation

> **Source:** NCDC; WHO; NHP
> **Evidence:** `../../evidence/sources.md`
> **Last verified:** 2026-10-28
> **Status:** Draft — clinician review required
> **Setting:** Rural OPD / PHC (India)

## 1. Quick facts

- Detect clusters early and report.
- Confirm diagnosis and define cases.
- Find source and mode of spread.
- Control and communicate.

## 2. Steps

1. Verify the outbreak
2. Define a case
3. Find cases (active surveillance)
4. Describe time/place/person
5. Form hypothesis
6. Test hypothesis
7. Control measures
8. Communicate and report

## 3. Diagnosis / Assessment

| Activity | Detail |
|---|---|
| Case definition | Clinical/lab criteria |
| Line list | Cases and exposures |
| Sampling | Water/food/stool |
| Lab testing | Confirm |

## 4. Treatment / control

| Measure | Detail |
|---|---|
| Isolation | Cases |
| Safe water | Chlorination |
| Hygiene | Handwashing |
| Vector control | Breeding sites |
| Paracetamol 500 mg | Symptomatic cases |

**Brand examples (illustrative):** ORS; Dolo 650.

## 5. Supportive care

- Community engagement
- Risk communication
- Logistics

## 6. Drug interactions and cautions

- **Antibiotics:** based on culture.
- **ORS:** safe.
- **Chlorine:** correct dosing.
- **Reporting:** timely.

## 7. Red flags — escalate

- Rapid spread
- Severe cases/deaths
- Unknown pathogen
- Contaminated water supply

## 8. Follow-up

- Monitor cases.
- Evaluate control measures.
- Final report.
