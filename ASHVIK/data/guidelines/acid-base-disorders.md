# Acid-Base Disorders

> **Source:** ICMR; WHO; NHP
> **Evidence:** `../../evidence/sources.md`
> **Last verified:** 2026-10-28
> **Status:** Draft — clinician review required
> **Setting:** Rural OPD / PHC (India)

## 1. Quick facts

- Use ABG to classify.
- Treat the cause, not just the number.
- Bicarbonate only in selected severe metabolic acidosis.
- Watch potassium in pH shifts.

## 2. Classification

| Disorder | pH | Primary change |
|---|---|---|
| Metabolic acidosis | ↓ | ↓ HCO3 |
| Metabolic alkalosis | ↑ | ↑ HCO3 |
| Respiratory acidosis | ↓ | ↑ PaCO2 |
| Respiratory alkalosis | ↑ | ↓ PaCO2 |

## 3. Diagnosis / Assessment

| Test | Use |
|---|---|
| ABG | pH, PaCO2, HCO3 |
| Anion gap | Metabolic acidosis type |
| Electrolytes | K+/Cl- |
| Lactate | Sepsis |
| Glucose/ketones | DKA |

## 4. Treatment

| Disorder | Treatment |
|---|---|
| DKA | Fluids, insulin, potassium |
| Lactic acidosis | Treat sepsis/shock |
| Metabolic acidosis severe | Sodium bicarbonate 50 mmol IV |
| Respiratory acidosis | Ventilation/refer |
| Metabolic alkalosis | Treat vomiting/electrolytes |

**Brand examples (illustrative):** Sodium bicarbonate; Insulin.

## 5. Supportive care

- Monitor ABG/electrolytes.
- Oxygen.
- Treat cause.

## 6. Drug interactions and cautions

- **Bicarbonate:** alkalosis, hypokalaemia.
- **Insulin:** hypokalaemia.
- **Diuretics:** metabolic alkalosis.
- **Paracetamol 500 mg** for associated pain.

## 7. Red flags — refer immediately

- pH < 7.20
- pH > 7.55
- K+ abnormalities
- Altered consciousness
- Shock

## 8. Follow-up

- Repeat ABG.
- Treat cause.
- Review medications.
