# Safe Abortion Care

> **Source:** MTP Act (India); WHO; NHP
> **Evidence:** `../../evidence/sources.md`
> **Last verified:** 2026-10-28
> **Status:** Draft — clinician review required
> **Setting:** Rural OPD / PHC (India)

## 1. Quick facts

- Abortion is legal under the MTP Act with consent and provider eligibility.
- Provide confidential, non-judgmental care.
- Medical or surgical methods depend on gestation.
- Contraception counselling after abortion.

## 2. Clinical features

- Missed period
- Nausea, breast tenderness
- Positive pregnancy test
- Abdominal pain/bleeding (complications)

## 3. Diagnosis / Assessment

| Test | Use |
|---|---|
| Pregnancy test | Confirm |
| Ultrasound | Gestation/location |
| Hb/blood group | Baseline |
| STI screening | As indicated |

## 4. Treatment

| Gestation | Method |
|---|---|
| ≤ 9 weeks | Medical (mifepristone + misoprostol) |
| ≤ 12 weeks | Surgical (MVA) |
| > 12 weeks | Refer to approved facility |

**Medical abortion:** mifepristone 200 mg oral, then misoprostol 800 mcg vaginal/buccal after 24–48 h.
**Pain:** paracetamol 500 mg.

**Brand examples (illustrative):** Mifepristone; Misoprostol — Cytotec.

## 5. Supportive care

- Counselling and consent
- Pain relief
- Contraception
- Follow-up

## 6. Drug interactions and cautions

- **Misoprostol:** cramping, fever, bleeding.
- **NSAIDs:** avoid in bleeding.
- **Rh-negative:** anti-D if indicated.
- **Ectopic pregnancy:** refer.

## 7. Red flags — refer immediately

- Heavy bleeding
- Fever/foul discharge
- Severe abdominal pain
- Suspected ectopic
- Incomplete abortion

## 8. Follow-up

- Review in 1–2 weeks.
- Contraception counselling.
- Mental health support.
