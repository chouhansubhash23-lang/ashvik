## THE ONE RULE THAT MATTERS MOST

> "A script must fully fit the positive features of the descriptor at a particular level.
> **Bolded text indicates negative features that will limit a rating.**"
> — ielts.org Writing Band Descriptors, footer of every descriptor page

Matlab: ek bhi **negative feature** jo us band ke descriptor mein bolded hai, aapko **us se neeche** gira degi. Half credit nahi milta.

## STEP 0 — Scoring architecture (pehle ye samjho)

| Fact | Detail |
|---|---|
| Writing ke 4 criteria | Task Achievement (T1) / Task Response (T2), Coherence & Cohesion, Lexical Resource, Grammatical Range & Accuracy |
| Weighting | **Har criterion equally weighted** |
| Task score | 4 criteria ka **average** |
| Task 1 vs Task 2 | Dono independently assess hoti hain. **Task 2 carries MORE weight** (double, per common practice) |
| Minimum words | T1 = 150, T2 = 250 |
| Underlength penalty | Kam words = higher bands ka evidence hi nahi milta → **automatically girta hai** |
| Writing report | Sirf whole ya half band (6.5, 7.0) |
| Speaking | 4 criteria: Fluency & Coherence, Lexical Resource, GRA, Pronunciation — equal weight |
| Listening / Reading | Descriptors nahi hote. 40 mein se correct answers → 9-band scale |

## STEP 1 — WRITING TASK 2 (Essay) CHECKLIST

### A. Task Response (TR) — 25%

| Band | Official key features |
|---|---|
| **9** | Prompt "appropriately addressed and explored **in depth**"; position clear + fully developed; ideas fully extended & well supported; lapses extremely rare |
| **8** | Prompt "appropriately and sufficiently addressed"; position "clear and well-developed"; ideas relevant, well extended, supported |
| **7** | Main parts addressed; clear & developed position; ideas extended & supported; **but** tendency to over-generalise, or lack of focus/precision in supporting material |
| **6** | Main parts addressed (**some less fully covered than others**); position relevant **but conclusions may be unclear, unjustified or repetitive**; some ideas insufficiently developed |
| **5** | Main parts **incompletely** addressed; position expressed **but development not always clear**; ideas limited; **irrelevant detail**; repetition |
| **4** | Prompt tackled "in a **minimal** way" or tangential; position discernible but **reader has to read carefully** to find it |

☐ Did I address **both** sides of a "discuss" prompt?
☐ Did I state a **clear position** (not just describe)?
☐ Is every main idea **extended + supported** (reasoning or example)?
☐ Is my conclusion a **justified new thought**, not a summary?
☐ Any irrelevant or repetitive content? (→ drags to 5)

### B. Coherence & Cohesion (CC) — 25%

| Band | Official key features |
|---|---|
| **8** | Logically sequenced; cohesion **well managed**; paragraphing sufficient & appropriate |
| **7** | Logically organised; clear progression; **a few minor lapses**; cohesive devices incl. reference & substitution used flexibly; **some inaccuracies or over/under use** |
| **6** | Generally coherent, clear overall progression; **cohesion may be faulty or mechanical** (misuse/overuse/omission); **paragraphing may not always be logical** |
| **5** | Organisation evident **but not wholly logical**; lack of overall progression; sentences **not fluently linked**; **paragraphing may be inadequate or missing** |

☐ Clear logical progression intro → body → body → conclusion?
☐ 4+ paragraphs, each with **one** topic?
☐ Varied cohesive devices (not just "Also / Moreover" repeated)?
☐ Any **mechanical/overused** linking words?
☐ Flexible reference & substitution (pronouns/articles), not endless repetition?

### C. Lexical Resource (LR) — 25%

| Band | Official key features |
|---|---|
| **8** | Wide resource, fluently & flexibly used for **precise meanings**; skilful use of **uncommon/idiomatic** items despite occasional word-choice & collocation inaccuracies |
| **7** | Sufficient for **some** flexibility & precision; **some** ability to use less common/idiomatic items; awareness of style & collocation evident, though inappropriacies occur |
| **6** | Generally adequate; meaning clear **in spite of a rather restricted range or lack of precision**; if risk-taker → wider range but **higher inaccuracy** |
| **5** | Limited but minimally adequate; **frequent** lapses in appropriacy of word choice; simplifications & repetition |

☐ Any **spelling errors**? (Note below ↓)
☐ Any topic-specific vocabulary, or is it all generic?
☐ Repetition of the same words/structure?
☐ Collocations natural ("boost establishing" ❌ → "boost the establishment of" ✓)?

> ⚠️ **Spelling & word formation sit inside LR**, not GRA. Frequent spelling errors = cap on LR even if grammar is perfect.

### D. Grammatical Range & Accuracy (GRA) — 25%

| Band | Official key features |
|---|---|
| **8** | **Majority of sentences error-free**; punctuation well managed; occasional non-systematic errors |
| **7** | **Variety of complex structures** used with some flexibility & accuracy; grammar/punctuation generally well controlled; **error-free sentences are frequent**; a few errors persist but don't impede |
| **6** | Mix of simple + complex, **but flexibility is limited**; complex structures **not marked by same accuracy as simple ones**; errors occur, rarely impede |
| **5** | Range **limited and repetitive**; complex sentences attempted but **faulty**; greatest accuracy on simple sentences; **grammatical errors frequent**, cause reader difficulty |

☐ Percentage of error-free sentences? Count them.
☐ Complex structures (relative, concessive, causative, conditional) — and are they *accurate*?
☐ Same accuracy in complex sentences as in simple ones? (Band 6 cap if not)
☐ Punctuation clean?

## STEP 2 — WRITING TASK 1 (Academic chart / GT letter)

Same 4 criteria, but **Task Achievement** replaces Task Response:

| Band | Key TA features |
|---|---|
| **7** | Overview **clear**, data **appropriately categorised**, main trends/differences identified; key features "could be more fully illustrated" |
| **6** | Overview **attempted**; key features covered and **adequately** highlighted; **some details missing or excessive** |

☐ **AC:** Overview statement present? (Missing overview alone = Band 5 max)
☐ **AC:** Grouped data, didn't just list every number?
☐ **GT:** All 3 bullet points covered? Clear purpose? Consistent tone?

## STEP 3 — THE PENALTY TRAPS (ye band girwa dete hain)

| Trap | Official consequence |
|---|---|
| **Underlength** | "Significantly underlength" → TR/LR/GRA drop; "Length may be insufficient to provide evidence of control of sentence forms" (Band 3 wording) |
| **Bullet points / note form** | Script "may be penalised if **not written as full, connected text**" |
| **Plagiarism** | "partly or wholly plagiarised" → penalised. **Copied rubric must be discounted** |
| **Memorised language** | "inappropriate use of lexical chunks (e.g. memorised phrases, formulaic language)" → drops LR |
| **Repeating the prompt** | "Possible over-dependence on input material or memorised language" |
| **Wrong language** | Band 0 |
| **No attempt** | Band 0 |
| **≤20 words** | Automatic **Band 1** |

## STEP 4 — HOW TO GIVE THE BAND

1. Score each of the **4 criteria** separately (don't do a gut feel on the whole essay).
2. **Sanity check**: does the essay *fully fit* the positive features of that band? Any bolded negative feature present? → **Go down a band.**
3. Average the 4 criteria → **Task 2 score**.
4. Do the same for Task 1.
5. **Task 2 counts double** → Writing score.
6. Report as **whole or half band** only.

---

## STEP 5 — OVERALL BAND (averaging 4 skills)

**Official rounding rule:**

> "If the average of the four sections ends in **.25**, the overall band score is rounded **up** to the next half band, and if it ends in **.75**, the overall band score is rounded **up** to the next whole band."

Worked examples straight from ielts.org:

| Test taker | L | R | W | S | Average | **Overall band** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | 6.5 | 6.5 | 5.0 | 7.0 | 6.25 | **6.5** |
| B | 4.0 | 3.5 | 4.0 | 4.0 | 3.875 | **4.0** |
| C | 6.5 | 6.5 | 5.5 | 6.0 | 6.125 | **6.0** |

## STEP 7 — SPEAKING (4 criteria, equal weight)

| Band | Summary |
|---|---|
| **9** | Fluent with **very occasional** repetition/self-correction. Hesitation only used to prepare next utterance |
| **8** | Fluent, only occasional repetition/self-correction. Demonstrates all Band 7 features **and some, but not all** of Band 9 |
| **7** | Fluent with **some** repetition/self-correction; **occasionally** hesitates; develops ideas with **coherence and some detail**; **occasionally slips**, but meaning generally clear |
| **6** | **Keeps going**, willingness to produce long turns; coherence sometimes lost due to hesitation/repetition/self-correction; **regularly slips**, but meaning is generally clear |
| **5** | Keeps going, but **unsteady**; coherence limited; **noticeable hesitation and repetition**; some grammar errors, but meaning generally clear |

## QUICK SCORING CHEAT-SHEET (yaad rakhne wali baat)

| Band | One-line test |
|---|---|
| **9** | Nothing to criticise |
| **8** | Only *occasional* slips |
| **7** | Good, but *some* errors / *lack of precision* remain |
| **6** | Gets the job done; *regular* mistakes; complex ≠ accurate |
| **5** | Many mistakes; reader needs effort |
| **4** | Barely addresses the task |

**Rule of thumb:** Spelling mistakes kill **LR**. Broken complex sentences cap **GRA** at 6. Missing/overused connectors cap **CC** at 6. Weak development caps **TR** at 5.
